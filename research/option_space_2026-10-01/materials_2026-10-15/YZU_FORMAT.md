# Yuan Ze University master's thesis format: rules checklist

Compiled 15 October 2026 from official Yuan Ze University (YZU) documents and the YZU thesis system hosted by the National Central Library (NCL).
The files are in `yzu_template/`. Source URLs and download times are in `yzu_template/README.md`.
Scope: an English-language master's thesis in the College of Management, MS in Finance and Accounting (財務金融暨會計碩士班), Finance track (財務金融碩士學程). This is the program on the 27 September proposal cover.

**Rule hierarchy.** The university guideline is a general rule. Departments and advisors may set field-specific conventions (G-zh p.5 註; G-en p.5 Note). No College of Management or program-level format guide was found (see "Not found" below). Confirm anything marked **ASK** with the advisor or the program office.

## 1. Language and the Chinese abstract

- [ ] **Language.** "The thesis or dissertation (including the abstract) must be written in Chinese as a rule" (以中文撰寫為原則). R17 Art. 12, p.5 (Chinese p.4). G-zh II.7, p.2 repeats "文章主體以中文為原則". G-en II.7, p.2 drops this phrase. No written exception for English theses was found. The program runs an English-taught track (GMS, MS114 p.12–13). **ASK** the program office to confirm in writing that an English thesis is accepted.
- [ ] **A Chinese abstract is mandatory, also for an English thesis.** "須有中文摘要及英文摘要，分頁書寫" (G-zh II.4, p.1). G-en: "Chinese and English Abstracts (one for each) ... Abstracts in Chinese and English should be done on separate pages" (G-en I.4 and II.4, p.1). The thesis system requires both a Chinese and a foreign-language abstract (UM-zh p.8: "中文／外文摘要，兩者皆需填入"; UM-en p.4: "abstracts in both Chinese and English").
- [ ] **A Chinese title is mandatory.** The title page carries the title in Chinese and English (G-en II.2, p.1; A2). The system requires both titles, and they must match the cover, abstract pages, approval sheet and authorization forms (UM-zh p.8).
- [ ] **Chinese keywords are mandatory in the system.** Chinese and foreign keywords are both required, one keyword per field (UM-zh p.8). The paper abstract templates have no keyword line (A5, A6).
- [ ] **Chinese reference list in the system.** The system says both Chinese and English reference lists must be filled in (UM-zh p.8: "中英文參考文獻，兩者皆需填入"). For a thesis with only English references, **ASK** the library (etds@saturn.yzu.edu.tw) what to enter.

## 2. Front matter and binding order

Current order (G-zh 一, p.1; G-en I, p.1):

1. [ ] Front cover (封面), A1
2. [ ] Title page / inside cover (書名頁), A2
3. [ ] Oral defense committee approval sheet (論文口試委員審定書), A3. A copy is bound in the paper thesis; the originals go to the department (G-en II.3, p.1). Do **not** put it in the uploaded PDF (personal data) (AA-98).
4. [ ] Chinese abstract, then English abstract, each on its own page (A5, A6)
5. [ ] Acknowledgements or preface (誌謝辭或序言), A8 (English version: heading "Acknowledgements")
6. [ ] Table of contents, A9 / A9-1 (choose one layout with the advisor, G-en III.1, p.2)
7. [ ] List of tables, A10
8. [ ] List of figures, A11
9. [ ] List of symbols (符號說明), A12. All mathematical and special symbols on one page, unnumbered (G-en III.3, p.3). The thesis uses V, R, W, D, m and g(·), so this page applies.
10. [ ] Main text with tables and figures
11. [ ] References
12. [ ] Appendices, A15
13. [ ] Back cover

- [ ] **Authorization forms are not bound.** Put them loose inside each paper copy (G-zh PS, p.1; G-en p.s., p.1; AA-98). They are generated by the thesis system (Appendix 4, AA-98).
- [ ] The 2013 order bound the authorization form and ended with a vita (自傳). The current order does neither (G-old vs G-zh, p.1). The Chinese TOC template still shows 自傳 (A9). Follow the current order.

## 3. Page, margins, paper

- [ ] A4, 210 × 297 mm (G-en II.5, p.1).
- [ ] Margins: top 3.5 cm, left 4 cm, right 2 cm, bottom 2 cm (G-en II.6, p.1; diagram p.2).
- [ ] Page number centred, 1 cm from the bottom edge (G-en II.6, p.1).
- [ ] Covers: paper of at least 150 lb (cloth-grain or card, G-zh II.5, p.1). Cover colour set by the department (G-en II.5, p.1). **ASK** the colour.
- [ ] Note: the downloaded appendix templates use Word default margins (2.54/3.17 cm). They show layout and wording only. Set the margins yourself.

## 4. Fonts and spacing

- [ ] **No font face or font size is set in the written rules** (G-zh, G-en, all pages). **ASK** the department.
- [ ] What the official templates use (observation, not a rule): Chinese text in 標楷體 (DFKai-SB); Latin text falls back to Times New Roman 12 pt (template default style). Cover: university name 24 pt, department 28 pt, degree line 24 pt, title 14 pt, student and advisor 18 pt (A1). Title page and abstracts 14 pt (A2, A5, A6). Page headings ("Acknowledgements", "Table of Contents", "List of Tables", "References", chapter title) 20 pt (A8-en, A9-en, A10-en, A13, A14-en). TOC entries 13 pt (A9-en).
- [ ] **English line spacing: 1.5 or double, at least 28 lines per page.** Double space under the chapter title (G-en III.4(3), p.3).
- [ ] Chinese text (the Chinese abstract): double-spaced, at least 32 lines per page, at least 28 characters per line, dense character spacing (G-en III.4(3)–(4), p.3; G-zh p.3).
- [ ] Text runs left to right, horizontally. Foreign-language originals quoted in the text go in parentheses (G-en II.7, p.2).

## 5. Page numbering

- [ ] Lower-case Roman numerals (i, ii, iii ...) from the title page to the lists of tables and figures. The title page and approval sheet are counted but carry no printed number (G-en II.8(1), p.2; G-zh 8(1), p.2).
- [ ] The templates number them: title page i, approval sheet ii, Chinese abstract iii, English abstract iv, acknowledgements v, contents vi, list of tables vii, list of figures viii, (symbols ix) (A9-en; A9). The cover is not numbered.
- [ ] Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3 ...) from Chapter 1 through the appendices, continuous (G-en II.8(2), p.2). References continue the main-text numbering (G-en III.5(1), p.4).
- [ ] In the PDF: no page number and no watermark on the cover (UM-zh p.9; CONV-en p.3, p.12–14). PDF page numbers must match the paper copy (CONV-en p.6, p.21).

## 6. Headings and text

- [ ] Chapters numbered 1, 2, ...; sections 1.1, 1.1.1, then 1.1.1 (1) (G-en III.4(1), p.3). English TOC template: "Chapter 1 Introduction", "2.1", "2.1.1" (A9-en).
- [ ] Chapter number and title centred at the top of the page. Sections and paragraphs start at the left margin, indented one or two spaces by level (G-en III.4(2), p.3; A13).
- [ ] Equations: set apart from the text and indented. Number them in parentheses at the right of the last line, e.g. "(1)". Cite them by name and number in the text. Break a long equation only at +, −, ×, ÷, >, <; align lines at "=". In running text write fractions with "/" (G-en III.4(6), p.3).
- [ ] Footnotes: superscript numbers, numbered from 1 again in each chapter. Separate from the text by a rule at the page bottom; may continue on the next page (G-en III.4(7), p.3).
- [ ] Numbers in text: the rule is written for Chinese (Chinese numerals for simple descriptive numbers; concise form for large ones, e.g. "Three point three billion US dollar" rather than 3,300,000,000) (G-en III.4(5), p.3).

## 7. Tables and figures

- [ ] Table number and title **above** the table (G-en III.4(9), p.4).
- [ ] Figure number and title **below** the figure (G-en III.4(9), p.4; the English text says "below tables", which is a slip; the Chinese says 圖號及圖名置於圖下方, G-zh p.4).
- [ ] Source and notes **below** both tables and figures (G-en III.4(9), p.4).
- [ ] Number tables and figures consecutively through the whole thesis, not by chapter: Table 1, Table 2 ... (G-zh 三.2 "不分章節連續編號", p.2; G-en III.2, p.2; A10-en, A11-en).
- [ ] Text inside tables and figures must be typed (G-en III.4(9), p.4).

## 8. Citations and references

- [ ] **University default:** number citations in order of first citation, in square brackets, e.g. "Duncan [1]"; the list follows that order (G-en III.4(8), p.4; III.5(1), p.4).
- [ ] Default reference formats (G-en III.5(2), p.4–5; A14-en):
  - Book (title italic): `Jelinek, F. (1968), Probabilistic Information Theory, McGraw-Hill, New York, pp. 79-82.`
  - Journal (journal italic, article title in quotes): `Pathak, S. and Kolhe, M. V. (2016), "Title", Journal, vol. 2, no. 9, pp. 19-24.`
  - Conference: `Huang, S. W. and Lin, S. W. (2008, Feb.), "Title", Conference Name, City, pp. 102-104.`
  - Thesis (title italic): `Pollard, A. (1978), Flow in Tee Junction, Ph.D. Thesis, University of London, London, U. K.` A Taiwanese thesis cited from NDLTD needs its handle URL (https://hdl.handle.net/...) (G-zh p.5).
- [ ] **Field convention allowed.** "Each advisor may specify the format of references according to the specific field of study" (G-en p.5 Note; A14-en). The Chinese text adds that departments may too (G-zh p.5 註). No College or program rule names APA or another style. **ASK** Prof. Kong to approve author-year (for example APA) before the reference list is finalised.
- [ ] References start on a new page after the main text (G-en III.5(1), p.4). Works not cited in the text go in an appendix list (G-en III.6, p.5).

## 9. Abstracts

- [ ] Content: purpose, data sources, methods and results (G-en II.4, p.1).
- [ ] Length: "approximately 500~1000 words within one page" (G-en II.4, p.1). The Chinese says 約500～1000字（並以一頁為限）, i.e. characters (G-zh p.1). **One page each is the binding limit.**
- [ ] Header block (A5, A6): title; student; advisor(s) ("Advisors: Dr. ..."); "Submitted to Department of ... College of ... Yuan Ze University"; then "ABSTRACT" (English) or 摘要 (Chinese).
- [ ] Keywords: no paper rule; required in the system in Chinese and English (UM-zh p.8). No limit on number found.

## 10. Cover, title page, spine

- [ ] **Cover (A1):** Chinese-only template: 元智大學; department (學系/所); 碩士論文; title; 研究生; 指導教授; date as 中華民國 ___ 年 ___ 月. Departments with MOE-approved tracks may add the track name (G-en II.1, p.1). The proposal cover used 管理學院財務金融暨會計碩士班 / 財務金融碩士學程. No English cover template exists.
- [ ] **Title page (A2):** Chinese title, English title; 研究生/Student; 指導教授/Advisor; 元智大學, department, 碩士論文; then "A Thesis Submitted to Department of ... College of Management, Yuan Ze University in Partial Fulfillment of the Requirements for the Degree of Master of Science in ..." (omit "Department of" in the last line); month and year; "Chungli, Taiwan, Republic of China."; 中華民國 年 月 (A2; G-en II.2, p.1). **ASK** the exact English department and degree names.
- [ ] **Spine (A17, `R-G-A18.doc`):** bound on the left. The spine strip shows graduation year (畢業級別), 碩士論文, title, school/college/department, author (G-en II.9, p.2; A17). Type it on white paper and copy it onto coloured spine paper chosen by the department; 3–4 strips per sheet (A17).
- [ ] **Approval sheet (A3):** bilingual form "Verification Letter from the Oral Examination Committee"; committee signatures, advisor, head of department; date in ROC years (A3). Bring two originals to the defense (AA-99; one to the department, one kept and copied into the bound thesis).

## 11. Defense and program rules (MS in Finance and Accounting)

- [ ] Advisor chosen and registered by the end of the first year, from the Finance, Accounting or Digital Finance groups (MS114 p.3, p.13).
- [ ] Proposal defense: apply within 2 weeks of the start of the planned graduation semester; hold it within 4 weeks; a failed proposal must be retaken and passed by week 8. Committee: advisor(s) plus one internal or external member. Only then may the final defense be requested (MS114 p.3, p.13).
- [ ] Research ethics course certificate before applying for the defense (R74 Art. 4, p.1; R17 Art. 4, p.2; MS114 p.3).
- [ ] Defense application includes the draft thesis and abstract and the advisor's recommendation letter (A16) (R17 Art. 4, p.1–2).
- [ ] Originality (similarity) report given to the advisor and committee on the defense day; the threshold is set by each department (R17 Art. 4(4), p.2). The program threshold was not found. **ASK**.
- [ ] Committee of 3–5 members; at least 3 present; one third from outside YZU; pass mark 70, averaged (R17 Art. 5, p.2; Art. 9, p.4). Defense announced publicly in advance (R17 Art. 9(1), p.4).
- [ ] English-taught track (GMS): defense by end of December (fall) or end of June (spring) (MS114 p.13).
- [ ] Submission deadline for the final thesis: end of January (fall) or end of July (spring) (R17 Art. 12, p.5).

## 12. Paper copies

- [ ] At least two bound copies (G-en II.10, p.2): one for the NCL and one for the YZU library; the department may ask for more (ETD-zh 四). Submit them to the department office after the upload is approved (ETD-en 2.2; WF).
- [ ] Each copy carries the loose authorization forms and, if any, the embargo application (WF; ETD-zh 四; NCL-up).
- [ ] The approval sheet copy is bound in (AA-98). The 2013 rule of five copies is superseded (G-old vs G-zh II.10).

## 13. Electronic submission (YZU thesis system on NCL)

- [ ] System: https://cloud.ncl.edu.tw/yzu/ ; only students graduating that semester may open an account; log in with the YZU portal account (UM-en p.2; UM-zh p.3).
- [ ] Step 1, metadata: basic data, Chinese and English abstracts, table of contents, references (UM-en p.4). Foreign name as in passport, surname first in capitals, e.g. "SUN, YAT-SEN"; list every committee member; graduation year must match the diploma; publication year must match the cover; page count = last page number (UM-zh p.8).
- [ ] Step 2, one merged PDF of the whole thesis (UM-zh p.9; REJ). Add page numbers and the YZU watermark (`ncl/YZU_watermark.png`) centred on every page except the cover, without resizing it (CONV-en p.3, p.7; REJ).
- [ ] PDF security: printing allowed (high resolution), changes not allowed, copying not allowed, no password to open (CONV-en p.17, p.21; UM-zh p.9). Do not use Word's "Save as PDF"; use Acrobat (CONV-en p.15) or PDFill (`ncl/PDFill_security_guide_en.pdf`). File name without Chinese characters or special symbols (CONV-en p.21). Fonts in the PDF must match the paper copy (CONV-en p.21).
- [ ] Do not include the approval sheet in the PDF (AA-98).
- [ ] Step 3: print two authorization forms (YZU and NCL), single-sided; the student and advisor sign by hand; scan and upload both; hand the originals to the department (UM-zh p.13, p.16; UM-en p.6). Foreign students sign their Chinese name and their passport English name (UM-zh p.13).
- [ ] Choose on-campus and off-campus open-access dates for the electronic full text (UM-zh p.9). Since academic year 108, paper theses are treated as immediately public (NCL-up).
- [ ] Step 4: submit for department review; edits need "cancel validation" (UM-en p.9–10).
- [ ] **Embargo:** allowed only for a patent application, national secrets, or legal restrictions on disclosure. Journal submission has not been a valid reason since academic year 109 (NCL-up). File EMB with evidence, signed by student, advisor and committee, stamped by the department; maximum 5 years per application; a second application needs the original committee or a department meeting to confirm (R17 Art. 4(5)–(6), p.2; EMB notes 1–3). If no date is set, the NCL releases the thesis at once (UM-en p.5). For "withheld by law", attach e.g. the confidentiality contract (UM-zh p.11). Relevant if licensed data (`LICENSED_DATA.md`) would appear in the thesis.
- [ ] Contact: etds@saturn.yzu.edu.tw, ext. 2322 (ETD-en).

## Not found

1. **No College of Management, MS in Finance and Accounting, or GMS format guide or Word template** was found online. 4. **No English cover template**; Appendix 1 is Chinese only.

6. **No program-chosen citation style** (APA or other); only the university's numbered default plus advisor discretion.
7. **No program similarity (Turnitin) threshold**, no program number of paper copies beyond two, no cover colour.
8. **No rule on keyword count, thesis length, landscape pages, or table font size.**
9. **How to fill the Chinese reference field** in the system when all references are English: not stated.
10. **YZU SharePoint material** (2026-05-28 upload briefing slides, similarity-check manual) needs a YZU login.
11. **NDLTD national submission rules beyond the YZU system:** the NCL deposit runs through the YZU system; the NCL embargo form is saved (`ncl/NCL_embargo_application_form.pdf`). The Degree Conferral Act itself (https://law.moj.gov.tw) was not downloaded because it is not a YZU or NCL document.
