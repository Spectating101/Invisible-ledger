(`www.yzu.edu.tw`) or from the YZU thesis system hosted by the National Central Library (NCL)
(`cloud.ncl.edu.tw/yzu/`, `nclfile.ncl.edu.tw`). Do not edit these files. Work on copies.

The rules extracted from these files are in `../YZU_FORMAT.md`.

## Folders

- `aa_format/`: Office of Academic Affairs format guidelines and Word templates (Appendices). `R-G-A00-new.doc` is the current Chinese guideline (saved 23 Nov 2023); `學位論文格式規範條例English.doc` is the current English guideline (saved 23 Nov 2023). `R-G-A00.doc` is the superseded 2013 Chinese version: it is still on the server but no longer linked; kept only for comparison.
- `aa_degree_exam/`: oral defense forms (approval sheet 審定書 = Appendix 3, recommendation letter = Appendix 16, score sheet).
- `regulations/`: YZU regulations 1-17 (degree examination rules, amended 24 Dec 2025, MOE noted 26 Jun 2026), 1-71 (academic ethics cases), 1-74 (research ethics course).
- `ncl/`: YZU electronic thesis (ETD) submission documents from the YZU thesis system on cloud.ncl.edu.tw, plus the NCL embargo form.
- `cm_ms_program/`: required-course lists of the MS in Finance and Accounting for entry years 113, 114, 115 (2024, 2025, 2026). They carry the program's thesis rules (advisor, proposal defense, deadlines).

Appendix map (Office of Academic Affairs numbering): 1 cover `R-G-A1`; 2 title page `R-G-A2`; 3 approval sheet `aa_degree_exam/審定書中英並列.doc`; 4 authorization form (generated by the thesis system, not downloadable); 5 Chinese abstract `R-G-A5`; 6 English abstract `R-G-A6`; 7 missing-character form `R-G-A7`; 8 acknowledgements `R-G-A8` (Chinese) and `R-G-A8(誌謝格式-英)` (English); 9 table of contents `R-G-A9`, `R-G-A9目錄編排範例格式-英`, `R-G-A9-1`; 10 list of tables `R-G-A10`, `R-G-A10表目錄範例-英`; 11 list of figures `R-G-A11`, `R-G-A11圖目錄範例-英`; 12 list of symbols `R-G-A12`; 13 chapter layout `R-G-A13`; 14 references `R-G-A14`, `R-G-A14參考文獻-英`; 15 appendix `R-G-A15`; 16 recommendation letter `aa_degree_exam/推薦函中英並列.doc`; 17 spine `R-G-A18`; plus `R-G-A19` (committee appointment letter) and `R-G-A20` (cancellation of degree examination).
