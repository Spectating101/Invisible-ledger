Per company
- Meituan: discloses amount yes; netted from revenue yes (IPO table “Reduction of revenue”; excess incentives also booked as selling and marketing expenses); years covered FY2015, FY2016, FY2017, 4M2017, 4M2018 from IPO. Later HKEX ARs 2019/2022/2024 discuss Transacting User incentives without restoring the netted-amount table (2019 Note 7 shows S&M Transacting User incentives RMB 8,149,976 thousand / 5,400,781 thousand for 2019/2018).
- Talabat: discloses amount yes; netted from revenue yes; years covered FY2022, FY2023, 9M2023, 9M2024 (IOM, AED) and FY2024, FY2025 (AR 2025, USD).
- Deliveroo: discloses amount no; netted from revenue unclear (incentives described; GTV defined net of discounts; no voucher amount); years checked IPO 2021.
- Just Eat Takeaway.com: discloses amount no; netted from revenue yes (discount vouchers recognised as a reduction to revenue when redeemed); years checked FY2024 AR (policy, no amount).
- Jumia: discloses amount no in the 20-F; netted from revenue yes (consumer incentives and marketplace subsidies recognised as a reduction of revenue); years checked FY2023 20-F. Quarterly earnings exhibits print Customer Incentives dollars but were not used because the spec asked for the 20-F.
- Ozon: discloses amount no; netted from revenue unclear (GMV stated net of discounts; no separate incentive line vs revenue); years checked FY2022 20-F.
- Mercado Libre: discloses amount no; netted from revenue unclear (shipping subsidies described; no amount); years checked FY2022 10-K.
- Farfetch: discloses amount no; netted from revenue yes (fulfilment revenue net of Farfetch-funded promotional incentives); years checked FY2022 20-F.
- Wish/ContextLogic: discloses amount no; netted from revenue yes (incentive discounts recorded as a reduction of revenue when redeemed); years checked FY2022 10-K.
- Lazada/Alibaba: discloses amount no; netted from revenue yes when consumer incentives are payments to customers under ASC 606, otherwise selling and marketing; years checked FY2024 20-F (year ended 31 Mar 2024). Lazada named as subsidiary; no Lazada-specific incentive amount.
- Coupang: discloses amount no; netted from revenue yes (promotional discounts reduce selling price and revenue); years checked FY2025 10-K.
