Company summaries:
- Eternal Ltd (Zomato): Customer incentives are partly netted from revenue (when the user is a customer / Group is responsible for delivery, to the extent of revenue from that user) and the excess is advertisement and sales promotion expense. Amounts netted from revenue are disclosed (INR 13 cr FY23, 194 cr FY24, 415 cr FY25 as discounts and consideration payable to customers); the expense line that includes remaining platform-funded subsidies is also disclosed.
- Swiggy Ltd: Customer incentives/discounts are recorded as a reduction of revenue when the user is identified as a customer, with the excess (and discounts where Swiggy is not responsible for the service) in advertising and sales promotion. A standalone customer-discount rupee total is not printed; Advertising and sales promotion (which the RHP says includes discounts and promotions) is disclosed, including as a % of B2C GOV for FY22–FY23. Delivery and related charges are explicitly said to include incentives paid to delivery partners.
- Nykaa: skipped (not a delivery/travel platform; spec said include only if easy).
