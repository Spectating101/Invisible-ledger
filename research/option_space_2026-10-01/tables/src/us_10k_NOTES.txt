UBER: no annual total of incentives recorded as a reduction of revenue in any FY2019–FY2025 10-K; Driver incentives reduce revenue (excess to cost of revenue); end-user discounts generally sales and marketing except market-wide promotions; UK agent-to-merchant change disclosed for 2022.
LYFT: annual totals of incentives recorded as a reduction to revenue printed for FY2019–FY2024 (not FY2025); YoY change in driver-supply investments recorded as a reduction to revenue printed for FY2021–FY2025.
DASH: promotions to consumers primarily recorded as a reduction of revenue in every FY2020–FY2025 10-K, but no annual dollar total of those promotions; Net Revenue Margin printed (Take Rate in earlier language); no stated pp/percent-of-volume incentive effect.
CART: coupons/incentives/appeasements recorded as a reduction of (transaction) revenue in FY2023–FY2025 10-Ks, but no annual dollar total and no printed transaction-revenue-as-%-of-GTV or pp margin effect.
