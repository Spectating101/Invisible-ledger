SERIES STATUS
- ecommerce_value: official DTI 2020 baseline and 2021-2022 targets; DTI citation of PSA 2017 $1.5B; BSP digital-payment shares/values (not GMV). No saved primary PSA national SICT/ASPBI/CPBI sales series for 2018-2025.
- ecommerce_businesses: DTI 2020 baseline 500,000 (platform-sourced) and BNRS 88,574 / 1,753; 2021-2022 are targets. No PSA establishment e-commerce count saved.
- ecommerce_value_channel: not found.
- sample_or_census: DTI says the 500,000 count is “based on initial information from platforms”; PSA sample/census note not saved.
- definition: DTI/PSA-OECD definition; BIR remittance exclusions; DOF digital-services list.
- digital_service_vat_collections: DOF estimates only (7.25 billion in 2025; 102.12 billion 2025-2029). No official BIR/DOF actual collection table found.
- platform_withholding_collections: RR 16-2023 rate 1% and PHP 500,000 threshold only. No yearly peso collections found.
- registered_foreign_providers: threshold PHP 3 million found; count not found in official sources.
- release_note: recorded per series used.

Business COUNTS and VALUE for the same years: DTI prints both a 2020 VALUE baseline (Php 599 B / $12 B) and a 2020 COUNT baseline (500,000 enterprises, plus 88,574 new online-store names). 2021-2022 DTI figures are targets for both series, not matched official outturns. PSA primary value and count for the same year were not obtained.
