SHOPEE INDONESIA ESTIMATES — CREDIBILITY RANKING

1. Most credible for replacing a modelled Shopee Indonesia series: Momentum Works Indonesia GMV share series (2022 36%, 2023 40%, 2024 46%, 2025 54%), applied only as shares, with MW Indonesia market GMV (2022 US$51.9 bn; 2024 US$56.5 bn; 2025 US$57.7 bn). MW is the only publisher found that repeatedly estimates Indonesia GMV by platform, defines GMV as paid app sales including cancelled/returned orders, and publishes a consistent annual series. Free texts do not print 2023 Indonesia-total GMV as 53.8 in English (Katadata prints US$ 53,8 miliar with a decimal comma); 2023 Indonesia total is implied by MW’s 46.9% of SEA US$114.6 bn but was not computed here.

2. Next: Google/Temasek/Bain e-Conomy SEA Indonesia e-commerce totals (2021–2025). These are the best country-level digital-economy e-commerce GMV, but they are sector totals (not Shopee) and they revise prior years (e.g. 2022 e-commerce $59 bn in the 2022 vintage vs $58 bn later; 2023 $62 bn then $59 bn; 2024 $65 bn then $62 bn; 2025 $71 bn vs a 2022-vintage $95 bn forecast). They cannot replace a Shopee series by themselves.

3. GoTo FY2022 E-Commerce GTV IDR 273 tn is the only company-reported Tokopedia-scale number found. It is not Shopee, uses GTV (not MW GMV), and is one year only.

4. Sea 20-F / earnings: most authoritative for Shopee group GMV definition (includes shipping and other charges; orders include unsettled/returned) and group GMV, but Sea does not disclose Indonesia GMV or Indonesia’s share of Shopee. Using group GMV as Shopee Indonesia would be wrong.

5. Least usable for a Shopee Indonesia level: Bank Indonesia quotes (Tirto Rp4.500 tn / 37 bn transactions) and Katadata’s computed US$ platform dollars (Indonesian decimal commas; some look like share × market). Bisnis.com’s US$31.2 bn / US$26 bn Shopee figures were not saved.

Do not multiply MW shares by e-Conomy or MW market GMV to invent a Shopee dollar series; those products are not printed in the saved texts (except where Katadata already printed a dollar figure, which was not used as the value field because of decimal-comma parsing).
