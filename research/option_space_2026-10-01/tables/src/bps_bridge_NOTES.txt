CONTRADICTIONS AND SCOPE GAPS
1. e-Conomy Indonesia e-commerce GMV is revised across vintages. 2023 report printed 2023 e-commerce as 62. 2024 report printed 2022=58, 2023=59, 2024=65. 2025 report printed 2023=59, 2024=62, 2025=71. Do not mix vintages without the source_file.
2. BI vs e-Conomy vs BPS. BI Cerita BI gives ~Rp487 tn e-commerce in 2024 (IDR payment-style series, rounded). BI LPI 2024 cites e-Conomy’s US$65 bn e-commerce for 2024. Those are different units and different concepts. BPS national IDR totals for 2022-2024 were not obtained here; news/Kemendag citations of BPS ~Rp1,100 tn (2023) and ~Rp1,289 tn (2024) were not copied because they are not first-hand BPS pages.
3. BI does not print 2022 or 2023 IDR e-commerce outturns in the saved LPI/Cerita/PTBI texts. Press reports of Rp476.3 tn (2022) and Rp453.75 tn (2023) were skipped as news.
4. GoTo e-commerce GTV 2022=Rp273.146 tn and 2023=Rp248.836 tn is the Tokopedia-era segment. From 1 February 2024 Tokopedia is deconsolidated; FY2024 Group GTV (Rp519.784 tn) is not Tokopedia GMV. 2024 GoTo GTV definition explicitly excludes Tokopedia.
5. Sea GMV includes shipping and other charges and is group-wide (SEA + other Shopee markets), not Indonesia. No returns/cancellations sentence was found next to the definition.
6. Bukalapak TPV 2024 is only through 30 September 2024; the company stopped reporting TPV from Q4 2024. Printed figures use dotted thousands (153.738.958) which this checker cannot treat as one integer, so values were left empty.
7. Blibli TPV is paid-and-delivered goods/services (not the same as GMV including unpaid/cancelled orders).
8. BPS survey unit (from Sulut/Bali pubs) is usaha/perusahaan, including household businesses and companies, formal and informal, using the OECD 2009 order definition. Marketplace is a third-party platform stall. Phone/manual-email/fax orders are out. The provincial books do not state whether recorded sales are gross of returns or include shipping/tax. Sulut sample (171 census blocks) is provincial, not the national frame (other BPS news pages mention 365 kabupaten/kota and ~40,066 sample businesses for 2024 listing, but those pages were not saved as the national publication).
9. e-Conomy e-commerce GMV is a sector separate from transport, food, online travel (OTAs only), and online media. The reports do not print a sentence on shipping, returns, or cross-border inside Indonesia e-commerce GMV.
10. Statistik E-Commerce 2022 (Dec 2022 release) covers 2021 activity, not calendar 2022. Calendar 2022 is in Statistik eCommerce 2022/2023.
