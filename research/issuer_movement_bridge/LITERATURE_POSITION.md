# Literature position for the proposed thesis spine

**Status:** targeted positioning memo, not an exhaustive systematic review.

The rebuilt thesis should not claim novelty for facts already well established: marketplaces report GMV separately from their own revenue; digital economies require multiple statistical perspectives; small online businesses can have weak conventional records; and third-party platform information can matter for administration.

The contribution has to come from the **specific Indonesian reconciliation and the economic inference it changes**.

## Proposition-by-proposition positioning

### P1 — broader e-commerce growth versus marketplace-only growth

**Existing knowledge:** BPS already measures Indonesian e-commerce from the business perspective and publishes channel, transaction-value and business-characteristic statistics. BPS has also published a separate analytical study of marketplace use. Therefore, neither the existence of nonmarketplace commerce nor the fact that messaging/social channels matter is a new discovery.

Primary BPS publications:
- <https://www.bps.go.id/en/publication/2025/01/30/d52af11843aee401403ecfa6/e-commerce-statistics-2023.html>
- <https://www.bps.go.id/en/publication/2025/11/28/647323224ecc656c2933571b/ecommerce-statistics-2024.html>

**Potential addition:** a definition-checked decomposition showing that the recent *growth story* changes materially when the analyst moves from a marketplace-only component to broader e-commerce and business participation. The novelty is the comparative inference, not the BPS totals themselves.

**Required claim form:**
> BPS establishes the breadth of channels; this study shows that using those channels changes the interpretation of recent aggregate growth in a specified way.

### P2 — transaction activity and recognized revenue can describe different movement

**Existing knowledge:** marketplace firms themselves treat GMV and take rate as distinct operating metrics. For example, eBay defines GMV as transaction volume and take rate as net revenue / GMV and uses the latter to identify revenue drivers. Therefore, the proposition that revenue and GMV are distinct objects is not novel.

External benchmark:
- eBay 2025 Form 10-K: <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065088/000106508826000027/ebay-20251231.htm>

**Potential addition:** a source-auditable Indonesian/platform-linked longitudinal comparison that measures how often the *direction and magnitude of movement* differ across candidate issuer/segment histories, instead of treating one ratio as a hidden-economy parameter.

**Required claim form:**
> Platforms distinguish scale and monetization conceptually; this study documents how consequential that distinction becomes for describing movement in selected Indonesia-linked platform histories.

### P3 — mechanisms behind divergence

**Existing knowledge:** incentives, take rates, product mix, principal-agent treatment and business-model choices can affect recognized revenue. These mechanisms are ordinary platform economics/accounting concepts.

**Potential addition:** source-linked mechanism cases where the observed movement is decomposed rather than merely asserted:
- Tokopedia FY2022→FY2023: higher gross revenue plus lower customer incentives;
- Blibli 3P FY2022→FY2023: higher segment revenue before direct promotion plus lower direct promotion;
- Blibli 3P FY2024→FY2025: issuer-documented shift toward higher-margin OTA categories and operating efficiency while TPV declines.

The paper should deliberately retain at least one unexplained reversal. That distinguishes observed evidence from mechanism storytelling.

### P4 — digital traceability versus institutional visibility

**Existing knowledge:** digital economics emphasizes reduced tracking/verification costs, and public-finance theory has long emphasized business records and third-party information. Statistical frameworks already distinguish intermediary transactions from the intermediary's own services.

Closest conceptual anchors:
- Goldfarb & Tucker (2019), *Digital Economics*, DOI 10.1257/jel.20171452.
- Kleven, Kreiner & Saez (2016), *Why Can Modern Governments Tax So Much?*, DOI 10.1111/ecca.12182.
- OECD (2023), *Handbook on Compiling Digital Supply and Use Tables*, DOI 10.1787/11a0db02-en.

**Potential addition:** an observer-specific Indonesian implementation showing which records are public, survey-estimated, legally specified for marketplace reporting, or still unlinked. PMK 37/2025 is especially useful because it specifies a seller-level information/reporting architecture distinct from platform corporate accounts.

The paper must leave open the possibility of successful institutional adaptation. Digitalization can create more traceability even while records remain partitioned across systems.

## Contribution standard

A final contribution paragraph should pass this template:

> **Prior work establishes X. This study uses source-matched Indonesian evidence to establish Y. Y changes the interpretation of Z.**

Examples of statements that are **too weak**:
- “GMV is larger than revenue.”
- “Indonesia has a large digital economy.”
- “Different ledgers exist.”
- “Most online businesses do not have financial reports.”

Examples of statements that could become **substantive if verified**:
- “A marketplace-only view implies materially different recent growth from the broader e-commerce view under comparable BPS definitions.”
- “Across accepted direct issuer/segment histories, corporate revenue frequently changes at a materially different rate from transaction activity, including source-explained sign reversals.”
- “Indonesia's emerging seller-level marketplace reporting architecture targets information that corporate revenue reporting does not contain, clarifying which institutional bridge is actually being built.”

## Literature-review stop rule

Do not add citations merely to make the bibliography longer. For each headline finding, identify:
1. the closest study/framework;
2. its estimand or object;
3. what it already establishes;
4. the exact additional observation or inference produced here.

If step 4 cannot be stated, demote the result rather than claiming novelty from packaging.
