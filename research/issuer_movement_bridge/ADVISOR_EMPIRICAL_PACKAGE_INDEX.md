## Scope certification

- **Tokopedia FY2022–FY2023:** strongest Indonesia-aligned direct segment; not a literal geographic line. Best core candidate pending advisor acceptance of an Indonesia-aligned operating segment.
- **Blibli 3P FY2020–FY2025:** direct and highly informative, but 3P includes online travel/OTA. Core only if the accepted research perimeter is broader than a pure goods marketplace.
- **Bukalapak Group FY2020–FY2023:** direct matched pairs, but Group has overseas operations with no recovered country allocation. Conservative treatment is supporting evidence unless geography is separately resolved.
- **Grab/Shopee:** conditional sensitivity only because country pairs contain derived and/or external quantities.

## Hard exclusions

- **Tokopedia FY2021:** full-year pro-forma transaction value paired with post-acquisition revenue.
- **Bukalapak FY2024:** 9-month TPV paired with 12-month revenue.
- **Blibli 3P FY2019:** retained as a direct period for coverage but has negative net revenue; not an ordinary positive-denominator ratio observation.

## Microdata status

The located SILASTIK file titled **Survei Usaha/Perusahaan E-Commerce 2024** is treated as **survey year 2024 / economic reference year 2023**, because its dictionary explicitly refers to online-selling conditions during 2023. It is therefore potentially excellent participant-side business-level evidence for the FY2023 platform comparison, but it does not create a 2023→2024 business panel.

Potential variables include financial-report ownership, named marketplace and nonmarketplace channels, marketplace/nonmarketplace revenue shares, transaction frequency, revenue growth, customer mix, exports, and survey weights.

## What Professor Kong needs to decide

The remaining central empirical decision is not whether more than one year exists. It is **which documented scope boundary is acceptable for the Indonesia longitudinal main analysis**:

- Tokopedia only is cleanest but too narrow;

- adding Bukalapak provides nine transitions but accepts a Group series with overseas operations;

The package is designed to let that decision be made from the data before any manuscript rewrite.
