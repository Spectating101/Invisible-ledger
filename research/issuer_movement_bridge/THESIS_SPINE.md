# Invisible Ledger — evidence-to-claim thesis spine

**Status:** research synthesis for deciding the final paper after sample approval. It is not a manuscript rewrite and does not override `docs/CURRENT_STATUS.md`.

The purpose is to prevent the rebuilt thesis from becoming either (a) a catalogue of datasets or (b) an accounting paper whose only result is that transaction value differs from revenue.

## Enduring question

> **What economically important features of Indonesia's digitally mediated commercial expansion become visible—or change interpretation—when transaction activity, platform-recognized revenue, business participation/records, and institutional information channels are examined as distinct but related evidence?**

The answer should be built from a small number of propositions with explicit evidence and limits.

## The affirmative thesis contribution if P1–P4 survive

A defensible contribution would be stronger than “there are multiple ledgers”:

> **Indonesia's digital commercial expansion cannot be described reliably by one firm-centred measure. Broader commerce, transaction activity, and recognized platform revenue can move differently; observed divergences are partly explained by incentives, monetization and business mix; and participant-level recordkeeping/reporting channels form separate information interfaces. Reconciling these interfaces changes how the depth and movement of the digital economy are interpreted.**

This is an economic-measurement and platform-finance result using accounting evidence, not a claim that accounting standards are wrong.

## Three memorable results to target

The final paper should ideally leave the reader with three results, not thirty modules:

1. **Breadth:** the recent growth story looks materially different when broader e-commerce channels/business participation are used instead of a marketplace-only lens.
2. **Movement:** within selected direct issuer/segment histories, recognized revenue can grow much faster, slower, or in the opposite direction from the transaction activity it accompanies.
3. **Mechanism/visibility:** incentives, margin/mix and reporting architecture explain why digital traces do not automatically collapse into one conventional corporate, merchant, statistical or tax ledger.

ASEAN/global evidence then answers a secondary question: **does this recur elsewhere, and under what heterogeneous institutional structures?**

## Stop rules

Do **not** promote a result because it has many rows, a low p-value in an ecological cross-section, or attractive prose.

Drop or demote any result that fails one of these tests:

1. **Source validity:** can the source → extract → analytic row → result chain be reproduced?
2. **Scope validity:** are geography, business perimeter, period and revenue/transaction definitions matched?
3. **Incremental information:** does the result tell us something beyond an already-published BPS statistic or the identity `revenue < transaction value`?
4. **Economic consequence:** does seeing the additional record change a concrete interpretation of participation, scale, growth, monetization or visibility?
5. **Literature position:** can the contribution be stated as `prior work establishes X; this study establishes Y; Y changes interpretation Z`?
