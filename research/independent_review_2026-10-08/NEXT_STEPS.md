## Direction

Complete the thesis from the existing research. Develop a focused journal article from that work afterward. Keep the approved title, question, hypotheses and Indonesia geography. Add research when it can resolve a material uncertainty in a central conclusion.

The contribution should explain when one economic measure can stand in for another. Revenue describes what platforms retain. Transaction value describes the sales they carry. National seller counts describe estimated participation. Changing take rates, channel coverage and survey participation can make their growth stories diverge. These mechanisms need separate evidence; they are not interchangeable counting errors.

The wedge quantifies the transaction–revenue separation. Its arithmetic alone is not the claim of novelty. The source-backed cases, explanations, reconciliation and conditions for using each measure carry the research contribution.

## Publication development

Use the thesis to establish the empirical argument. Then prepare one article with one central contribution: the conditions under which revenue and participation measures support a growth conclusion, tested through the Indonesian evidence. Keep the thesis title and question intact; any article framing is a separate later decision.

Before choosing a journal, compare this argument with the closest primary literature. Identify what the study adds beyond established take-rate arithmetic, gross/net revenue recognition and survey discontinuities. A case with well-documented mechanisms and economic consequences can justify publication without a new causal design. A broader measurement contribution needs a method or set of conditions that another researcher can apply.

A specialist Indonesia economics outlet is the natural first route if the country findings carry the article. A measurement outlet becomes stronger if the reusable reconciliation method carries it. This handoff sets no submission decision or acceptance probability. Resolve the documented data-use conditions before external reuse of licensed material.
