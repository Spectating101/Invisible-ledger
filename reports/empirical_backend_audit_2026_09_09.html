Reproduction conclusion¶

The backend now supports a much richer measurement study than the original three-row workbook, but it does not create one giant homogeneous sample. The defensible architecture is modular: Indonesia-focused multi-year issuer/segment evidence, conditional country reconstructions, source-reconciled quarterly accounting records, official BPS business statistics, and an exploratory market layer. Advisor approval is still required for the main-sample boundary.
