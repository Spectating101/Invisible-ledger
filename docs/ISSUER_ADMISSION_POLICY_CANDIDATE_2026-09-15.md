# Invisible Ledger — issuer admission policy candidate

**Date:** 2026-09-15  
**Status:** `RESEARCHER_CANDIDATE / ADVISOR_RATIFICATION_REQUIRED`  
**Authority effect:** none until explicitly ratified. `docs/CURRENT_STATUS.md` remains authoritative for what has or has not been advisor-approved.

## 1. Decision this note resolves

The remaining issuer-sample problem should not be framed as a binary choice between:

1. pretending Blibli 3P and Bukalapak Group are strict Indonesia-only observations; or
2. discarding their direct multi-year evidence because their disclosed business/geographic perimeters are broader than strict Indonesia goods-only commerce.

Both choices lose information.

The recommended policy instead separates **inference domain** from **source directness**. A direct issuer series may be admitted to longitudinal within-series analysis without being promoted to an Indonesia-only country observation.

This preserves Indonesia as the proposed main geography while preventing business-perimeter and geography caveats from being laundered away.

## 2. Proposed evidence architecture

### Domain A — Indonesia-specific evidence

Use for claims that are explicitly about Indonesia as a geography, national market, participant population, payment system, or administrative system.

Core families:

- BPS national and business-level evidence;
- Bank Indonesia payment-system evidence, with payment/e-commerce definitions kept distinct;
- Indonesian institutional/tax-administration evidence;
- Tokopedia e-commerce as the strongest **Indonesia-aligned direct issuer segment**, with the explicit caveat that it is a segment rather than a literal geographic country line.

No Blibli 3P or Bukalapak Group level may be described as an Indonesia-only monetary observation under this policy.

### Domain B — direct issuer longitudinal translation

Use for the RQ1 measurement question: whether issuer-disclosed transaction activity and recognized revenue provide materially different within-series accounts of platform growth.

Admit the currently certified direct issuer/segment candidates, subject to the existing period/source rules:

| Series | Periods | Positive-base annual transitions | Proposed use | Scope label that must remain visible |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Tokopedia e-commerce | FY2022–FY2023 (2) | 1 | Direct Indonesia-aligned mechanism/growth case | `direct_segment / Indonesia-aligned, not literal country line` |
| Blibli 3P | FY2019–FY2025 (7) | 5 | Direct multi-year within-series growth translation | `direct_segment / includes OTA-travel and changing business mix` |
| Bukalapak Group | FY2020–FY2023 (4) | 3 | Direct multi-year within-series growth translation | `direct_group / geography broader than strict Indonesia due overseas operations` |

Inventory: **13 direct periods / 9 positive-base annual transitions**.

This is a direct-evidence longitudinal set, **not** a pooled Indonesia panel and **not** 13 Indonesia country observations.

### Domain C — conditional country reconstruction

Grab Indonesia and Shopee Indonesia remain sensitivity / historical-continuity evidence because matching country transaction value and revenue require allocation, derived, or external-estimate assumptions.

They may be used to test whether a result is qualitatively sensitive to the conditional country constructions. They must not be promoted into the direct-evidence longitudinal set.

### Domain D — external corroboration

ASEAN histories, global issuer histories, company-wide accounting histories, and other external modules remain separate corroboration layers. They may establish recurrence or heterogeneity but never validate an Indonesia country allocation or enlarge an Indonesia sample N.

## 3. Proposed admission rule

An issuer-period may enter **Domain B direct issuer longitudinal translation** only when all of the following hold:

1. transaction and revenue measures cover the same economic period;
2. transaction and revenue measures refer to a sufficiently stable common issuer/segment perimeter for within-series comparison;
3. geography and business scope are known well enough to label, even when broader than strict Indonesia;
4. currency, units, definitions, original/restated/pro-forma status, and material structural breaks are recorded;
5. any derived component is transparent and separately classified;
6. the observation is not a duplicate annual/quarterly/vintage representation of the same economic period;
7. the resulting claim is restricted to the disclosed series' inference domain.

A broader-than-Indonesia or broader-than-goods-only perimeter therefore does **not** automatically exclude a direct issuer series. It changes the claim the series is allowed to support.

## 4. Geography rule

Indonesia remains the proposed main geography.

Under this candidate policy:

- `Indonesia-wide`, `Indonesia national`, `Indonesian businesses`, `Indonesian payments`, or `Indonesia administrative visibility` claims require Domain-A evidence appropriate to that unit;
- `issuer-disclosed activity and revenue diverge within series` may use Domain B;
- Blibli must never be silently relabelled `Indonesia goods e-commerce`; its 3P perimeter includes online travel through tiket.com and changing mix;
- Bukalapak Group must never be silently relabelled `Indonesia-only`; overseas operations remain part of the disclosed group perimeter;
- Tokopedia remains `Indonesia-aligned segment`, not a literal geographic line.

The thesis may therefore remain Indonesia-centred without pretending that every issuer accounting series is a country statistic.

## 5. Revenue-basis rule

Do **not** force one cross-platform revenue concept merely to make the column names look harmonized.

The admissible comparison is primarily **within series**. Select the issuer-defined revenue measure that:

1. is available on a consistent basis across the retained periods;
2. corresponds to the same disclosed business perimeter as the transaction measure as closely as source evidence permits;
3. preserves gross/net/service/principal-agent definition changes explicitly;
4. flags restatements, acquisition/deconsolidation, incentive presentation, and mix changes rather than overwriting them.

Cross-platform **levels** remain descriptive and scope-labelled. Cross-platform summaries of within-series growth divergence may be reported only as diagnostics of the admitted evidence, not as a common-level panel estimate.

## 6. Hard exclusions that remain unchanged

- **Tokopedia FY2021:** exclude because transaction and revenue periods do not match.
- **Bukalapak FY2024:** exclude because transaction value and revenue cover different durations.
- Missing matched periods remain missing; no interpolation or annual-total division by four.
- Overlapping annual, quarterly, cumulative and publication-vintage representations are not independent observations.

## 7. Allowed headline after ratification

Recommended:

> Across the direct issuer/segment longitudinal evidence used in the Indonesia-centred study, transaction activity and recognized revenue frequently imply materially different within-series growth stories. The evidence is deliberately scope-labelled: it is not a pooled Indonesia country panel.

Allowed diagnostic wording:

> The current direct-evidence inventory contains 13 economic periods and 9 positive-base annual transitions across Tokopedia e-commerce, Blibli 3P and Bukalapak Group. These series are compared within their own disclosed perimeters rather than pooled as common Indonesia levels.

## 8. Wording that remains prohibited

Do not write:

- `13 Indonesia platform-years` without the direct-issuer/scope qualifier;
- `9 Indonesia panel transitions`;
- `Blibli Indonesia e-commerce` when the cited 3P perimeter includes OTA/travel;
- `Bukalapak Indonesia` for Group-level values that include overseas operations;
- `representative Indonesian platform sample`;
- `Indonesia-wide wedge` derived from summing issuer transaction/revenue differences;
- any claim that the direct issuer set estimates population, GDP, undeclared income, tax loss, compliance, or causal effects.

## 9. Relationship to the existing 42.94pp diagnostic

The existing **9-transition / 42.94 percentage-point median absolute growth-divergence** result is compatible with this policy only as a **Domain-B direct-evidence diagnostic**.

It must not be re-labelled as an Indonesia population estimate or country effect. Its value is to show that non-equivalence recurs across multiple directly disclosed issuer/segment histories even when those histories have different labelled perimeters.

The all-tier **17 levels / 12 transitions / 42.02pp** result remains a broader inventory diagnostic because it also includes conditional Grab/Shopee constructions.

## 10. Why this is preferable to the three binary boundary options

### Tokopedia only

Maximum geographic discipline, but only two direct levels / one transition. It cannot support a meaningful multi-year issuer longitudinal chapter by itself.

### Tokopedia + Blibli as if both were Indonesia-only

Adds the strongest long history but overstates the geographic/business precision of Blibli 3P and hides the travel/mix issue.

### Tokopedia + Blibli + Bukalapak as one Indonesia panel

Maximizes direct periods but incorrectly implies a common country/perimeter unit across three materially different disclosures.

### Proposed policy

Retains all direct multi-year information **without pooling the units or upgrading their geography**. The cost is more explicit labels; the benefit is a larger longitudinal evidence base with honest inference boundaries.
