> My thesis is titled *The Invisible Ledger: Quantifying the Invisible Wedge in Indonesia's Platform Economy*. It measures the difference between the transaction value digital platforms process in Indonesia and the revenue those platforms recognize for themselves, examines how that difference moves over time and what explains it, and then considers what the underlying transaction records mean for statistical and tax-administrative reporting. The empirical work uses Indonesian issuer disclosures, official BPS e-commerce statistics, and Indonesia's recent marketplace withholding regulation.
