## 1. Canonical result hierarchy

The thesis currently supports five result families, in descending order of narrative importance:

1. **Issuer growth non-equivalence:** transaction activity and recognized revenue can provide materially different accounts of platform growth.
2. **Mechanism reconciliation:** incentives, monetization, accounting presentation, and business perimeter explain part of the divergence in source-auditable cases.
3. **Broader-economy translation:** national e-commerce growth is associated with both business-participation growth and sharply different marketplace/non-marketplace trajectories.
4. **Uneven traceability and institutional visibility:** structured marketplace participation is associated with stronger conventional records, but private digital traces only become administratively useful through additional legal and operational linkage.
5. **External corroboration:** payment, ASEAN, and global issuer evidence show that digital traces and transaction/revenue relationships evolve under different definitions and institutions; they support recurrence and scope discipline, not a common pooled effect.

The old FY2023 three-platform residual remains a lineage/sensitivity result and is not the thesis headline.

## 3. Core longitudinal result: activity and revenue can tell different growth stories

Two valid summaries exist because they answer different evidence-admission questions.

### 3.1 All-tier inventory diagnostic

Across the labelled all-tier inventory of **17 matched annual candidate levels** and **12 within-series transitions**:

- revenue grows faster in **10** transitions;
- transaction activity grows faster in **2**;
- **2** transitions have opposite signs;
- median absolute transaction/revenue growth divergence = **42.02 percentage points**.

This includes direct Indonesia-aligned, direct scope-pending, and conditional country evidence. It is not a final pooled Indonesia sample.

### 3.2 Direct-candidate scope sensitivity

Restricting to currently transcribed direct issuer/segment candidates yields **9 direct transitions**:

- revenue grows faster in **6**;
- transaction activity grows faster in **3**;
- **3** have opposite signs;
- median absolute divergence = **42.94 percentage points**.

The three opposite-sign transitions are:

- Tokopedia FY2022->FY2023: transaction value **-8.90%**, selected net revenue **+53.20%**;
- Blibli FY2020->FY2021: transaction activity **+14.86%**, net revenue **-28.08%**;
- Blibli FY2024->FY2025: TPV **-1.89%**, net revenue **+12.07%**.

**Interpretation:** the result establishes non-equivalence of growth signals in the candidate evidence. It does not estimate a representative platform-population effect.

## 4. Mechanism evidence: disagreement can reveal economic structure

The analysis should not stop at detecting divergence. Current source reconciliations establish several mechanisms.

### 4.1 Tokopedia FY2022->FY2023

- transaction value: **-8.90%**;
- third-party net segment revenue: **+53.20%**;
- of the arithmetic increase in net revenue:
  - **60.56%** is associated with lower customer incentives;
  - **39.44%** is associated with higher gross revenue.

This is an arithmetic component reconciliation, not a causal decomposition. It establishes that stronger recognized revenue did not require equivalent expansion in facilitated transaction activity.

### 4.2 Blibli FY2022->FY2023

The **Rp926bn** increase in net revenue reconciles to approximately:

- **Rp664bn** higher pre-promotion revenue;
- **Rp262bn** lower discount/direct promotion.

### 4.3 Blibli FY2024->FY2025

- TPV: **-1.89%**;
- net revenue: **+12.07%**;
- GPBD: **+13.93%**.

The issuer attributes GPBD improvement mainly to higher-margin OTA mix and operating efficiency.

### 4.4 Mechanism unresolved case

Blibli FY2020->FY2021 remains an observed sign reversal whose mechanism is not yet closed in the current source review.

**Canonical interpretation:** transaction/revenue disagreement can reflect monetization, incentives, principal/agent presentation, business mix, acquisition/deconsolidation, geographic/segment perimeter, or publication basis. There is no single universal "hidden" quantity behind the divergence.

## 5. BPS national result: broader digital growth has a different anatomy

### 5.1 2023->2024 national growth

Certified BPS evidence gives:

- total nominal e-commerce transaction value: **Rp1,100.87T -> Rp1,288.93T = +17.08%**;
- estimated e-commerce businesses: **3,816,750 -> 4,400,972 = +15.31%**;
- implied nominal value per estimated business: **+1.54%**.

This is an aggregate arithmetic decomposition. It is not a causal estimate of firm entry, productivity, real output, or welfare.

### 5.2 Marketplace vs non-marketplace component

Using directly reported BPS amounts:

- marketplace: **Rp200.68T -> Rp203.58T = +1.45%**;
- non-marketplace: **Rp900.19T -> Rp1,085.35T = +20.57%**;
- total: **+17.08%**.

Of the **Rp188.06T** increase in total nominal e-commerce value, approximately:

- marketplace increase = **Rp2.90T**, about **1.54%** of the total increase;
- non-marketplace increase = **Rp185.16T**, about **98.46%** of the total increase.

**Canonical interpretation:** recent BPS-measured e-commerce expansion is not adequately summarized by the marketplace component alone. The result does not imply that non-marketplace activity is untaxed, informal, or unrecorded elsewhere.

## 6. BPS business-level result: conventional recordkeeping differs by channel

BPS's published business-level analysis of reference-year 2023 compares **6,148 marketplace users** with **33,588 non-marketplace users**.

Published differences include:

- legal entity status: **17.40% vs 7.68%**;
- complete financial statements: **28.63% vs 12.25%**;
- IT training: **8.49% vs 2.83%**;
- cash use: **37.74% vs 83.29%**.

BPS's multivariate logistic model reports an odds ratio of **1.8463** for financial-statement ownership as a predictor of marketplace use, with **95% CI 1.7083-1.9955** and **p<0.001**.

**Boundary:** this is an official association, not a causal marketplace-formalization effect. Selection, firm size, capability, or other factors may explain part of the relationship.

### 6.1 Province evidence is a negative aggregation result

The province layer does **not** recover a stable marketplace/financial-report relationship:

- 2024 Pearson r approximately **0.022**;
- within-province 2023->2024 Pearson r approximately **0.309**;
- weighted within-province r approximately **0.099**.

The province result is best retained as an ecological warning: aggregate marginals do not reliably recover the business-level relationship.

## 7. Bank Indonesia payment result: digital traces grow under different definitions

Bank Indonesia adds an independent payment-system observation layer:

- **18,954** national monthly SPIP metric-period rows;
- **42,701** regional monthly rows;
- **1,460** published annual rows through 2024;
- separate official QRIS observations for 2023-2025.

These are not e-commerce sample observations and must never be added to issuer/BPS counts.

### 7.1 2023->2024 growth comparison

- BPS total e-commerce value: **+17.08%**;
- BPS marketplace component: **+1.45%**;
- BPS non-marketplace component: **+20.57%**;
- SPIP electronic-money shopping value: **+30.47%**;
- SPIP mobile-banking payment/purchase value: **+82.84%**;
- QRIS transaction value: **+186.98%**.

**Canonical interpretation:** payment traces, sales-media estimates, and issuer measures observe different actions and populations. Rapid growth in a payment trace does not validate or measure equivalent growth in e-commerce, platform GMV, merchant revenue, GDP, or taxable income.

### 7.2 Definition dependence inside one authoritative source

Bank Indonesia's own 2023 publications show that similarly named quantities can have different scopes. For electronic money, the headline total sits between the SPIP all-component and shopping-only quantities. This is a definition cross-check, not evidence of error.

## 8. Institutional result: a legal seller-reporting bridge exists, operational closure does not

PMK 37/2025 establishes a legal architecture under which designated marketplaces collect, deposit, and report Article 22 income tax associated with domestic sellers' PMSE income.

The framework distinguishes seller-linked turnover from marketplace corporate revenue and includes seller identifiers such as NPWP/NIK, address fields, turnover declarations, withholding, deposit, and reporting roles.

DJP appointed Blibli, Shopee Indonesia, Tokopedia, and Lazada on 1 July 2026, then postponed application through 31 October 2026 and cancelled/reissued the initial implementation sequence.

**Established:** legal reporting architecture exists.

**Not established:** actual transaction coverage, taxpayer matching quality, data use, compliance effects, enforcement effects, or complete linkage across platform, payment, BPS, and tax records.

The correct visibility sequence is therefore:

`record exists -> identity available -> legally reportable -> transmitted -> matched -> operationally usable -> outcome observed`

Current evidence closes only some of these stages.

## 9. ASEAN and global corroboration

### 9.1 ASEAN

The revision-aware ASEAN module contains **450 source-vintage rows** yielding **42 latest-vintage country-years** across six countries.

Latest-vintage e-commerce GMV rises between 2023 and 2025 in all six retained country histories, but platform structures, regulations, and tax architectures differ materially.

Publication-vintage sensitivity is substantial: **45 of 60** repeated cells change between earliest and latest publication, with median absolute revision among changed cells of approximately **7.14%**.

Use: recurrence, heterogeneity, and measurement-vintage discipline.

Do not use: pooled ASEAN treatment effect, common monetization rate, or validation of Indonesia allocations.

### 9.2 Global issuers

The global panel contains:

- **48 matched issuer-years** across eight platform businesses;
- **40** consecutive transitions;
- **11** known scope-break flags;
- **29 clean-screened transitions**.

Among the 29 clean transitions:

- revenue grows faster in **22**;
- transaction activity grows faster in **7**;
- **4** move in opposite directions;
- median absolute divergence = **7.10 percentage points**.

Use: evidence that transaction/revenue non-equivalence recurs across materially different business models.

Do not use: representative global population inference or validation of Indonesia country allocations.

## 10. What is established together

The current empirical record supports the following bounded answer:

> Large digital-commercial growth does not translate into one conventional economic measure. It appears differently as facilitated transaction activity, issuer monetization, business participation, channel composition, payment-system activity, statistical estimates, and legally reportable information. These records can imply materially different conclusions because they measure different economic objects. Reconciliation can therefore reveal where growth is occurring, how it is monetized, how broad participation is, and what additional linkage is required before digital traces become institutionally usable.

This is the current empirical basis for the *Invisible Ledger* concept.

## 11. Claims that remain outside the evidence

The project does **not** currently establish:

- missing GDP or national value added;
- undeclared merchant/participant income;
- unpaid tax or tax evasion;
- an Indonesia-wide transaction-minus-revenue total;
- representative population effects for all platforms or countries;
- causal marketplace formalization;
- causal investor losses or mispricing;
- completed PMK policy effects;
- institutional inability to link records;
- record-level linkage between payment, issuer, merchant, BPS, and tax datasets;
- welfare loss caused by fragmented observability.

These are possible future questions, not present findings.

## 12. Proposal-ready result set

For the thesis proposal, the main results should be compressed to four evidence blocks:

1. **Issuer growth divergence:** direct-candidate transitions include sign reversals; Tokopedia is the cleanest mechanism example.
2. **BPS growth anatomy:** national e-commerce growth is closely accompanied by business-count growth, while non-marketplace activity grows far faster than the marketplace component.
3. **Participant visibility:** marketplace users have stronger published conventional recordkeeping and lower cash reliance, association only.
4. **Institutional traceability:** BI and PMK show that abundant digital traces and legal reportability are distinct from a unified economic or administrative record.

Everything else belongs in robustness, context, or appendix.
