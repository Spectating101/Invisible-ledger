## Recommended decision

Approve the following inference architecture for RQ1:

> Use Tokopedia e-commerce, Blibli 3P, and Bukalapak Group together as a **scope-labelled direct issuer longitudinal evidence set for within-series transaction-versus-revenue growth**, while reserving strict Indonesia geographic claims for Indonesia-specific/national modules and explicitly **not** treating the 13 direct periods as one Indonesia country panel.

## Why this decision is needed

The direct issuer evidence is strong enough for longitudinal within-series analysis, but the three disclosures do not have identical geographic/business perimeters:

| Series | Direct periods | Positive-base transitions | Scope issue |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Tokopedia e-commerce | 2 | 1 | strongest Indonesia-aligned segment, but not a literal geographic country line |
| Blibli 3P | 7 | 5 | includes online travel/OTA through tiket.com and changing business mix |
| Bukalapak Group | 4 | 3 | group values include overseas operations, so they are broader than strict Indonesia |
| **Total direct inventory** | **13** | **9** | **must remain scope-labelled, not pooled as Indonesia levels** |

Rejecting Blibli/Bukalapak entirely would discard the strongest direct multi-year histories. Calling all three strict Indonesia observations would overstate what the filings disclose.

The proposed rule retains the information while keeping the inference boundary explicit.

## What approval would mean

### RQ1 direct longitudinal set

Admit the 13 direct periods / 9 positive-base transitions above for **within-series** comparison of transaction activity versus recognized revenue.

The current direct-evidence diagnostic remains:

- revenue growth faster in 6 of 9 transitions;
- transaction growth faster in 3 of 9;
- 3 opposite-sign transitions;
- median absolute transaction/revenue growth divergence: **42.94 percentage points**.

These are non-equivalence diagnostics, not population effects.

### Indonesia-specific claims

Keep Indonesia-specific/national claims separate and supported by the relevant unit:

- BPS national/business evidence;
- Bank Indonesia payment evidence;
- Indonesian institutional/tax-administration evidence;
- Tokopedia as the strongest Indonesia-aligned direct issuer segment, with its segment caveat visible.

Blibli 3P and Bukalapak Group would **not** become Indonesia-only monetary observations.

### Conditional evidence

Grab Indonesia and Shopee Indonesia remain sensitivity/historical-continuity evidence because their matched country transaction/revenue series require derived/allocation/external-estimate assumptions.

### Revenue basis

Do not force a single synthetic revenue definition across companies. Compare each direct issuer series on the most stable issuer-defined revenue basis available across its retained periods, while explicitly recording gross/net/service/principal-agent changes, restatements, acquisitions/deconsolidations, and other structural breaks.

## What approval would **not** mean

It would **not** authorize claims that:

- the 13 periods form a representative Indonesia platform panel;
- Blibli 3P is a pure Indonesia goods-e-commerce series;
- Bukalapak Group values are Indonesia-only;
- the 42.94pp diagnostic estimates GDP, missing GDP, tax loss, evasion, compliance, or a causal effect;
- BPS, Bank Indonesia, issuer, ASEAN/global, and administrative modules can be added into one N;
- Grab/Shopee have become direct-core observations.

## Existing exclusions remain

- Tokopedia FY2021 remains excluded because transaction and revenue periods do not match.
- Bukalapak FY2024 remains excluded because transaction value and revenue cover different durations.
- Missing periods remain missing; no interpolation.
- Annual, quarterly, cumulative, and publication-vintage duplicates are not independent observations.
