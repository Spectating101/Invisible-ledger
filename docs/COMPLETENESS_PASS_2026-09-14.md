## What was added

**§2.3 Measuring the digital economy.** The national-accounting frameworks that separate the
buyer–seller transaction from the intermediation service and reject treating gross transaction value as
value added (Ahmad and Schreyer 2016; IMF 2018; OECD 2023; SNA 2025). This was the most serious
omission: the thesis is a measurement paper whose central non-claim is *the wedge is not value added*,
and that non-claim previously rested on the author's own assertion rather than on the literature that
establishes it. All four references were already in the repository.

**§5.4 Payment traces and administrative records.** Bank Indonesia's payment statistics, merged to
`main` but never used in the proposal. Verified against
`outputs/payment_ledger_2026-09-11/bps_payment_growth_comparison_2023_2024.csv`: BPS e-commerce value
+17.08%, electronic-money shopping +30.47%, mobile-banking payment/purchase +82.84%, QRIS +186.98%.
The point is that digital traces expand on a different trajectory from the commerce they record, so
trace abundance is not itself evidence of measured activity.

**§6 Why the Problem Matters.** The stakes. Different records support different diagnoses of the same
transformation, and the risk is using one as a proxy for another without reconciling the boundary.

**Table 5, planned work.** Restored from the 13-page version; the 9-page had only a paragraph.

## Efficiency pass

Two paragraphs were cut as genuinely redundant once §6 existed — §1.3's second paragraph and §5.5's
second paragraph, 128 words, both restating the institutional boundary that §6 now states better.
Table 2's column grid was fixed so that four cells stopped wrapping to two lines, and back-matter
leading was tightened. A reference-ordering error was corrected: United Nations sorted before Ulyssea.

## Why it lands at 10 and not 9

Nine pages was tested and is not reachable honestly. The document carries 3,877 words, and eight
content pages plus a title page would require **485 words per page** — well above Flashpoint's 420,
which is already dense. Shrinking Figure 1 from 7.9cm to 4.0cm was tried and does not change the page
count, so the figure is not the binding constraint; total volume is. Reaching 9 would mean cutting
roughly 400 further words of substance, which fails the stated threshold that every restoration must
answer a documented advisor concern, clarify an empirical boundary, or improve the oral-defence story.

Final: **10 pages, A4, 5 tables, 1 figure, validates clean**, inside the 9–11 range agreed as
acceptable. Density is 387 words per page against Flashpoint's 420, with the remaining difference in
heading and equation leading rather than in dead space.
