# Lazada and TikTok Shop: admission feasibility

**Status:** source-feasibility assessment as of 11 September 2026. Neither platform is admitted to the Indonesia issuer transaction/revenue sample by this note.

## Decision

Lazada and TikTok Shop can materially improve the **market-activity, competitive-coverage, and institutional-change** evidence. They cannot currently be added as equivalent observations to the issuer transaction/revenue panel because a matching Indonesia revenue series has not been located for either platform.

| Platform | Indonesia transaction evidence | Matching Indonesia platform revenue | Current admissible role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lazada | External country GMV/share estimates for multiple years | Not separately disclosed by Alibaba; Lazada is combined with AliExpress, Trendyol and other international-commerce businesses | Market coverage, competitive structure, regional corroboration |
| TikTok Shop | External Indonesia GMV estimates for 2021–2023; later combined market shares with Tokopedia | ByteDance does not publish TikTok Shop Indonesia revenue; GoTo's post-transaction fee is not the combined entity's revenue | Market growth, 2023 policy break, post-merger public-visibility case |

## Expanded material retained

The collection now preserves three noninterchangeable tables:

| File | Rows | Unit | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `data/longitudinal/lazada_tiktok_indonesia_market_activity_candidates_2021_2025.csv` | 9 | Indonesia platform/combination-year | Lazada and TikTok-specific admission inventory |
| `data/asean_context/indonesia_platform_market_share_snapshots_2022_2025.csv` | 22 | Indonesia platform-year market snapshot | Competitive structure and value-coverage calculations |
| `data/asean_context/sea_leading_platform_gmv_2020_2024.csv` | 19 | Southeast Asia platform-year | Regional transaction-activity corroboration |

These row counts must not be added to the issuer sample N. The tables overlap economically and differ in geography, platform perimeter, and purpose.

The reported platform shares are rounded. Consequently, the 2024 and 2025 platform shares sum to 101% rather than 100%; the rows preserve the source values and must not be renormalized silently.

## Lazada

### What can be sourced

- Momentum Works estimates reported by Katadata assign Lazada 10% of Indonesia's US$51.9 billion platform e-commerce GMV in 2022, or approximately US$5.19 billion.
- The corresponding 2023 estimate is 9% of US$53.8 billion, or approximately US$4.84 billion.
- The repository's direct Momentum Works 2025 market table reports Lazada at 6% of Indonesia's US$57.7 billion platform e-commerce GMV.
- Regional Momentum Works material reports Lazada GMV of approximately US$21.0 billion in 2021 and US$20.1 billion in 2022.

These are useful transaction-market observations. They are external estimates rather than Lazada issuer disclosures.

### Why Alibaba revenue cannot be paired with them

Alibaba reports an International Commerce Retail business containing Lazada, AliExpress, Trendyol, Daraz, Miravia, and related direct-sales and logistics activity. Its FY2024 annual report presents International Commerce Retail revenue of RMB43.679 billion, RMB50.933 billion, and RMB81.654 billion for fiscal 2022–2024, respectively. These are not Lazada-only, Indonesia-only, or calendar-year figures.

Pairing Indonesia Lazada GMV with Alibaba International Commerce Retail revenue would therefore mix:

- one country with many countries;
- one platform with several platforms;
- calendar years with March fiscal years;
- marketplace, direct-sales, customer-management, and logistics revenue.

That pair must not enter the analytical panel.

### What would change the decision

Lazada becomes eligible only if a primary PT Ecart Webportal Indonesia or Lazada entity source supplies transaction value and a matching revenue measure for the same period and business perimeter. Seller fee schedules are not a substitute for recognized revenue because realized advertising, logistics, direct-sales, discounts, refunds, and other components remain unknown.

## TikTok Shop

### What can be sourced before the Tokopedia combination

- Momentum Works reports that Indonesia was TikTok Shop's initial Southeast Asian market and attributes approximately US$0.6 billion of GMV to the platform in 2021.
- Momentum Works reports approximately US$2.5 billion of TikTok Shop GMV in Indonesia in 2022.
- The 2023 Indonesia market estimate assigns TikTok Shop 11% of US$53.8 billion, or approximately US$5.92 billion.

This produces a useful pre-combination activity series of three annual estimates. It does not produce a matching revenue series.

### The 2023–2024 structural break

TikTok Shop halted Indonesian transactions in October 2023 following the social-commerce rule change. The TikTok Shop Indonesia and Tokopedia businesses were combined under PT Tokopedia on 31 January 2024, with TikTok holding the controlling interest. Post-combination figures therefore cannot be treated as a continuation of standalone TikTok Shop Indonesia.

GoTo reports receiving an e-commerce service fee from Tokopedia after the combination:

- approximately Rp110 billion for February–March 2024;
- Rp171 billion gross in 2Q24;
- Rp191 billion gross in 3Q24;
- Rp690 billion gross, or Rp622 billion excluding VAT, for eleven months of FY2024;
- Rp820 billion for FY2025.

This is **GoTo's contractual revenue from Tokopedia**, not PT Tokopedia's revenue and not TikTok Shop's monetization. It should be analyzed as a change in what the listed parent publicly recognizes after deconsolidation, not divided into combined ShopTokopedia GMV as though it were platform revenue.

### What would change the decision

TikTok Shop becomes eligible for the pre-combination transaction/revenue panel only if ByteDance or a local entity source provides a matching Indonesia revenue series. The post-combination entity becomes eligible only if PT Tokopedia provides both transaction activity and a consistently defined revenue series. External GMV plus a seller fee schedule does not meet that standard.

## What these platforms add without forcing admission

### 1. Better market coverage

Adding their GMV/share estimates makes the market denominator explicit. In 2023, the five named platforms—Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop, Lazada, and Blibli—account for 94% of the rounded Momentum Works estimate. The research can therefore distinguish large value coverage from direct accounting coverage.

### 2. A useful missing-disclosure result

Lazada represents 9% of estimated 2023 Indonesian marketplace GMV and TikTok Shop represents 11%, yet neither exposes a matching public Indonesia revenue series. Their absence from the issuer panel is not arbitrary selection; it is an empirical feature of the public information environment.

### 3. A strong perimeter-change case

TikTok Shop's suspension and combination with Tokopedia demonstrate why legal entity, platform identity, consolidation, and calendar period must be resolved before interpreting growth. GoTo's later service-fee disclosure shows that public visibility can shrink or change even when commercial activity continues in a controlled affiliate.

### 4. Tax-policy alignment

DJP's initial 2026 marketplace design designated Lazada alongside Blibli, Shopee, and Tokopedia. TikTok Shop was not separately named in that first group, while its Indonesian commerce was operating through the combined PT Tokopedia structure. This supports an institutional mapping of legal collectors; it does not establish the economic coverage of every brand-facing storefront.

### 5. Longitudinal competitive evidence

The regional series adds five annual observations for Lazada (2020–2024) and four for TikTok Shop (2021–2024). Lazada's estimated regional GMV rises from US$13.1 billion in 2020 to US$21.0 billion in 2021, then declines gradually to US$18.0 billion by 2024. TikTok Shop rises from US$0.6 billion in 2021 to US$22.6 billion in 2024. These paths document a major reallocation of marketplace activity, but they do not identify revenue, profitability, seller welfare, or a causal competitive effect.

The Indonesia snapshots show Lazada's estimated share declining from 10% in 2022 to 7% in 2024 and 6% in 2025. Standalone TikTok Shop rises from 5% in 2022 to 11% in 2023 and 2024; the 2025 source reports TikTok Shop and Tokopedia together at 38%, so that final row is not a continuation of the standalone series.

## Recommended paper treatment

```text
INDONESIA MARKET ACTIVITY LAYER
  Shopee + Tokopedia + TikTok Shop + Lazada + Blibli
  -> market shares, GMV, concentration, structural change

INDONESIA ISSUER ACCOUNTING LAYER
  only platform-periods with matched transaction and revenue measures
  -> longitudinal divergence and mechanism analysis

DISCLOSURE-GAP RESULT
  large platforms missing from the second layer
  -> evidence about public measurement coverage, not zero activity
```

The paper should report both **value coverage** and **paired-measure coverage**. It should not improve the latter by constructing unsupported revenue values.
