## Relative interpretation

### CL–ECI

Treat **CL–ECI as the current flagship benchmark** unless a later explicitly dated comparison supersedes this note.

Its advantage is not simply score magnitude. It is the combination of:

- conceptual architecture;
- methodological novelty;
- falsifiable tests;
- technical / system implementation;
- unusually tight integration between theory, evidence, and built artifact.

The prior conclusion remains:

> **CL–ECI is the strongest overall research invention / flagship artifact.**

It was also previously described as the strongest finished paper because its argument, tests, and built system lock together.

### Invisible Ledger

IL should no longer be benchmarked as merely a wedge paper.

The current framing is:

> **a measurement-sufficiency / proxy-validation framework for determining when observable digital-economy indicators are reliable proxies for the underlying economic activity people use them to represent.**

Its comparative strengths are:

- empirical depth;
- source and admission discipline;
- mechanism reconciliation;
- robustness architecture;
- conventional thesis / paper legibility;
- a now-clear generalizable measurement contribution.

Current IL may now match CL–ECI in overall maturity depending on final consolidation, but it does so through a different route. CL–ECI remains stronger in conceptual / methodological invention; IL is stronger as a conventional empirical-finance / measurement thesis candidate.

### FCP

FCP remains the cleaner, tighter institutional paper.

Its comparative strengths are:

- narrative economy;
- source closure;
- institutional mechanism clarity;
- falsifiability;
- compactness and defensibility.

Compared with current IL, FCP is less empirically deep and less broad analytically, but it remains the cleaner paper in structural terms.
