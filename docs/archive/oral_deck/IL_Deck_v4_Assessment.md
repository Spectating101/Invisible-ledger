# Deck v4: assessment

Every page is checked against the rules you set:

1. Open on a real, zoomed-out story.
2. No numbers or technical detail early.
3. Literature comes before the research question.
4. Slides are material to speak from, not exposition.
5. Visuals are limited and big elements stay modest.
6. The subtitle states the finding.
7. Content sits tight under the heading.
8. Neutral tone that still makes the stakes clear.
9. Proposal stage, not a finished thesis.

Each page also has a job and gets a verdict.
