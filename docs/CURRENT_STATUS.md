# Current status — 9 September 2026

## Advisor-facing decision state

The proposed main geography is Indonesia. The one-year FY2023 comparison is source-auditable, but it is not sufficient by itself as the master's-thesis sample. The immediate empirical task is to establish the longest coherent multi-year Indonesia series that can be supported without mixing incompatible scopes.

No expanded sample is represented here as advisor-approved.

## What is already defensible

- FY2023 source chain for Grab, Tokopedia and Shopee, with direct versus derived inputs stated explicitly.
- Historical Grab and Shopee filing extracts.
- A 47-row company-quarter accounting inventory with source-reconciliation files.
- Seven Blibli annual issuer-year candidates from 2019–2025, subject to segment and geographic eligibility.
- Four Bukalapak annual group candidates from 2020–2023, subject to overseas-operation and segment-scope checks.
- Grab country-revenue coverage and additional Shopee country-market anchors; these do not independently provide matching country transaction value and revenue.
- Preserved market prices for five platforms and three benchmarks, sufficient to revisit—but not automatically validate—the exploratory event study.
- ASEAN and World Bank context kept in a separate data layer.

## Primary unresolved decisions

1. Which platform/segment/year records form the longitudinal Indonesia main sample?
2. Is an Indonesia-aligned issuer segment acceptable when it is not an explicit geographic line?
3. Which revenue basis is consistent across time: gross revenue, net revenue, service revenue, or another issuer-defined measure?
4. How should Blibli's online-travel component and Bukalapak's overseas operations affect admission?
5. Should Grab and Shopee country allocations remain conditional cases rather than core observations?
6. Is the resulting measurement/disclosure analysis sufficient, or is an additional empirical test required?

## Do not currently claim

- that the main sample N is finalized;
- that the selected platforms represent the entire Indonesian platform economy;
- that the transaction–revenue difference is a national total;
- that the difference measures missing GDP, undeclared income or unpaid tax;
- that the event study is final;
- that an ASEAN aggregate validates Indonesia-specific assumptions.
