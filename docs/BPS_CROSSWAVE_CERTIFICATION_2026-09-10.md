# BPS cross-wave source certification — 10 September 2026

## Purpose

This note certifies which 2023–2024 BPS e-commerce comparisons can be treated as source-concordant descriptive comparisons and which still require questionnaire-level work. It does not imply causal identification.

## 1. Transaction value and sales-media decomposition

### 2023

BPS *E-Commerce Statistics 2023* reports **E-Commerce Transaction Value and Percentage of E-Commerce Transaction Value by Sales Media, 2023**:

- total: **Rp1,100.87T**;
- marketplace/platform digital: **Rp200.68T / 18.23%**;
- non-marketplace/platform digital: **Rp900.19T / 81.77%**.

Official publication:
<https://www.bps.go.id/en/publication/2025/01/30/d52af11843aee401403ecfa6/e-commerce-statistics-2023.html>

### 2024

A BPS-Statistics Indonesia presentation, **Measuring the E-commerce Value in Indonesia** by the Directorate of Services Statistics, reports **E-Commerce Transactions Value by Sales Media, 2024**:

- total: **Rp1,288.93T**;
- marketplace: **Rp203.58T / 15.79%**;
- non-marketplace: **84.21%**;
- total growth relative to 2023: **17.08%**.

BPS presentation hosted by UN SIAP:
<https://www.unsiap.or.jp/sites/default/files/doc/2026-04/08_Measuring%20E-Commerce%20in%20Indonesia_Webinar%20on%20Digitalization_update1.pdf>

Official BPS 2024 publication:
<https://www.bps.go.id/en/publication/2025/11/28/647323224ecc656c2933571b/statistik-e-commerce-2024.html>

## 2. Certified descriptive comparison

Both source layers frame the national split as e-commerce transaction value **by sales media**. Use the directly reported 2024 marketplace amount rather than reconstructing it from the rounded share.

| Component | 2023 | 2024 | Growth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Marketplace | Rp200.68T | Rp203.58T | **+1.45%** |
| Non-marketplace | Rp900.19T | Rp1,085.35T | **+20.57%** |
| Total | Rp1,100.87T | Rp1,288.93T | **+17.08%** |

This establishes that the published non-marketplace component grew substantially faster than the marketplace component between 2023 and 2024.

## 3. Interpretation boundary

This remains a comparison of published national aggregate survey estimates. It does not identify the same enterprises across years, entry/exit, productivity, causal channel substitution, tax status, hidden activity, or issuer GMV/TPV.

## 4. Financial-report ownership

The published values are:

- 2023: **15.19%**;
- 2024: **17.15%**.

The values themselves are established. A strong behavioral interpretation of the +1.96pp movement remains guarded until the exact questionnaire wording, skip logic and estimation universe are matched across waves.

## 5. Relation to Box's hypothesis execution

Box's first hypothesis script reconstructed the 2024 marketplace component from the rounded 15.79% share and therefore reported approximately +1.42% marketplace growth and +20.58% non-marketplace growth. The direct BPS 2024 amount, Rp203.58T, refines those to **+1.45%** and **+20.57%**. The difference is a rounding/source-precision correction, not a contradictory economic result.
