# Why Invisible Ledger matters

## Central proposition

The existence of several ledgers is not itself a failure. Platforms, companies, merchants, statistical offices, payment providers, and tax authorities legitimately record different objects for different purposes. Fragmentation becomes economically consequential only under a more demanding condition:

```text
one ledger is used as a proxy for another
                 ×
the two ledgers diverge materially
                 ×
a real decision depends on the distinction
                 ×
the difference cannot be reconciled reliably
```

This **decision-relevance test** supplies the stakes missing from a simple “records are scattered” narrative.

The paper should therefore not argue that every difference among ledgers is harmful. It should identify the decisions for which substitution across ledgers produces a different conclusion and document whether a bridge exists.

## The problem in one example

Tokopedia FY2022–FY2023 provides the clearest demonstration:

- transaction value fell approximately 8.90%;
- net third-party segment revenue rose approximately 53.20%; and
- approximately 60.6% of the arithmetic net-revenue increase is associated with lower customer incentives, while 39.4% is associated with higher gross revenue.

A transaction-based observer sees contraction. A revenue-based observer sees strong expansion. Both measurements can be correct because they measure different parts of the commercial system.

The analytical problem is not that one number is fraudulent. It is that conclusions about growth, market health, monetization, and merchant activity depend on which number is substituted for “the business.”

## Stakes hierarchy

### Stake 1 — incorrect diagnosis of platform performance

**Decision affected:** investor, analyst, lender, manager, or researcher assessment of whether a platform's underlying commercial activity is expanding.

**Evidence in this project:**

- the direct Indonesia candidate evidence contains opposite-sign transaction/revenue movements;
- the global corroboration module finds four opposite-sign movements among 29 clean-screened annual transitions; and
- issuer definitions and business-perimeter changes materially affect revenue-to-transaction comparisons.

**Why it matters:** platform revenue can improve while coordinated transaction activity declines, or vice versa. Revenue growth can reflect monetization, reduced incentives, advertising, logistics, subscriptions, principal-versus-agent treatment, or a changed business perimeter. It is therefore unsafe to interpret revenue growth automatically as equivalent commercial-activity growth.

Financial-statement comparability is decision-relevant beyond this sample. De Franco, Kothari, and Verdi (2011) find that greater comparability is associated with greater analyst following and forecast accuracy and lower forecast dispersion.^1 SEC staff guidance likewise warns that non-GAAP measures may be misleading when they are not clearly labelled or when gross/net presentation is inconsistent with the relevant accounting role.^2 IFRS 15's principal-agent framework bases gross-versus-net presentation on whether the entity controls the specified good or service before transfer.^3

**Boundary:** the present paper does not establish investor losses, security mispricing, or forecasting effects for these platforms. It establishes the prerequisite measurement divergence and explains mechanisms. The investor consequence is literature-supported relevance, not an executed outcome test.

### Stake 2 — policy aimed at the wrong commercial channel

**Decision affected:** whether policy, business-support, formalization, or reporting initiatives directed at marketplaces describe the wider e-commerce economy.

**Evidence in this project:** between 2023 and 2024, BPS reports total Indonesian e-commerce value rising 17.08%, marketplace value rising only 1.45%, and the non-marketplace component rising 20.57%. The 2024 exclusive decomposition places 15.79% of value in the marketplace component.

**Why it matters:** a marketplace-centred policy may address important and highly concentrated platforms while still observing only one sales channel. Websites, messaging applications, social commerce, and other non-marketplace channels can follow a different growth path. A conclusion based exclusively on listed marketplaces can therefore misdiagnose where digital-commercial expansion occurs.

This is not merely hypothetical in the project data. Marketplace value and non-marketplace value produce sharply different growth narratives during the same national period.

**Boundary:** the BPS channel decomposition does not establish that non-marketplace activity is untaxed, informal, or invisible to every institution. It establishes that marketplace records are not a complete substitute for a national all-channel e-commerce measure.

### Stake 3 — inability to use transaction traces for proportionate tax administration

**Decision affected:** seller identification, pre-filling, withholding, compliance checks, administrative burden, and parity between covered online and offline activity.

**Evidence in this project:** PMK 37/2025 legally distinguishes merchant transaction-linked turnover from platform corporate revenue and establishes seller-identity, withholding, deposit, and reporting responsibilities for designated marketplaces. DJP describes the mechanism as an administrative change rather than a new tax.^4 The initial implementation was postponed through 31 October 2026, so operational performance is not yet observable.^5

The wider literature explains why the bridge can matter. Kleven et al. (2011) find very low evasion for third-party-reported income compared with self-reported income in Danish audit data.^6 Pomeranz (2015) shows that VAT paper trails create preventive deterrence and enforcement spillovers in Chile.^7 The OECD's platform-reporting rules require covered operators to collect seller identity and consideration information and report it to tax administrations.^8

**Why it matters:** a transaction can be digitally traceable to a platform while remaining administratively unusable if the relevant seller identity, amount, period, and reporting rule are not linked. Conversely, an operating reporting bridge can make the transaction more legible without changing platform corporate revenue at all.

**Boundary:** the present evidence does not show that Indonesian merchants evade tax, that records fail to match in practice, or that PMK 37/2025 improves compliance. The established stake is that the legal architecture treats linked seller-turnover information as administratively valuable. Effectiveness is a future empirical question.

### Stake 4 — mismeasurement of the breadth and composition of digital transformation

**Decision affected:** statistical priorities, SME programmes, infrastructure planning, digital-policy evaluation, and interpretation of whether growth is concentrated among incumbents or broadened across businesses.

**Evidence in this project:** from 2023 to 2024, estimated Indonesian e-commerce transaction value rose 17.08%, estimated participating businesses rose 15.31%, and implied nominal value per estimated business rose 1.54%.

**Why it matters:** an issuer-revenue view cannot distinguish growth created by greater participation from growth created by larger activity per business. Those two patterns imply different accounts of economic transformation and potentially different policy needs.

International measurement institutions recognize this problem. The OECD states that existing metrics struggle to keep pace with digital transformation and recommends more granular data, data-linking opportunities, and carefully governed use of private-source data.^9 UNCTAD's statistical manual treats e-commerce value measurement, business ICT surveys, data sources, and quality control as distinct statistical tasks.^10 The IMF distinguishes the core digital sector from the increasingly digitalized economy and identifies platform classification, cross-border activity, and intermediation-fee information as measurement obstacles.^11

**Boundary:** the arithmetic decomposition does not identify firm entry, survival, productivity, real value added, welfare, or causality. It establishes that participation and value per estimated business provide different descriptions of aggregate growth.

### Stake 5 — policy and research decisions based on a revisable past

**Decision affected:** market benchmarking, cross-country comparison, target setting, trend narratives, and evaluation against historical baselines.

**Evidence in this project:** 45 of 60 repeated ASEAN country-year metric cells change between earliest and latest publication vintages; among changed cells, the median absolute revision is approximately 7.14%. Issuer comparative vintages also change, although the known Blibli FY2023 difference is not load-bearing.

**Why it matters:** a country may appear larger, smaller, faster-growing, or closer to a target depending on the source vintage used. Treating successive estimates as interchangeable can turn a methodological revision into apparent economic growth or conceal a real change.

**Boundary:** a revision is not evidence of poor faith or failed statistics. Later information and improved methods can legitimately improve estimates. The research stake is auditability: users need to know which historical vintage generated a conclusion and whether the conclusion survives revision.

### Stake 6 — unequal visibility and compliance burden across businesses

**Decision affected:** whether reporting systems privilege businesses already capable of maintaining formal records and whether obligations can be applied proportionately.

**Evidence in this project:** BPS's published business-level analysis reports complete financial statements for 28.63% of marketplace users and 12.25% of non-marketplace users, alongside substantial differences in legal-entity status and cash use. BPS's adjusted model reports a positive association between financial-statement ownership and marketplace use.

**Why it matters:** digital transaction traceability and conventional business recordkeeping are not the same capability. A reporting mechanism can be technically available while smaller or less formal merchants face different compliance capacity. Conversely, more formal businesses may select into marketplaces precisely because they already possess those capabilities.

**Boundary:** this is association, not causal marketplace formalization. The project does not yet estimate compliance costs, exclusion, or distributional welfare effects.

## Benign difference versus consequential fragmentation

| Situation | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Transaction value and revenue differ, but the user understands both definitions | Benign specialization of records |
| The measures differ and an issuer supplies a stable reconciliation | Manageable comparability problem |
| The measures imply different growth directions and the mechanism can be decomposed | Economically consequential but explainable divergence |
| A marketplace rule observes one channel while faster growth occurs elsewhere | Coverage boundary relevant to policy design |
| Private transaction traces exist but institutional access or matching is not established | Potential linkage problem requiring evidence |
| Different source vintages change the historical baseline | Measurement-governance and auditability problem |
| A residual is labelled missing GDP or unpaid tax without participant or administrative linkage | Invalid inference created by substituting ledgers |

This distinction prevents the thesis from treating ordinary institutional specialization as inherently bad.

## Strongest paper claim

> Digitalization increases the quantity and granularity of transaction records, but it does not produce one automatically integrated economic ledger. In Indonesia, platform activity, issuer revenue, participating businesses, sales channels, and administrative reporting rules provide materially different views of digital-commercial change. These differences become consequential when one view is used to assess another: issuer revenue can reverse the growth signal from transaction activity, marketplace evidence can underdescribe faster-growing non-marketplace commerce, and tax administration requires seller-linked turnover records that corporate revenue does not contain.

This claim has identifiable stakes without asserting that the entire difference is economically missing.

## Research contribution after adding stakes

The contribution has three parts:

1. **Diagnostic:** identify when transaction, revenue, participant, statistical, and administrative ledgers are being substituted for one another.
2. **Empirical:** measure cases in which the substitution changes the growth or coverage conclusion and reconcile the mechanisms where disclosures allow.
3. **Institutional:** identify which linkage would be required for investor interpretation, official measurement, or tax administration, and distinguish legal availability from operational evidence.

The paper is therefore not merely documenting scattered files. It studies **decision-relevant non-equivalence among economic records**.

## Development priorities

### 1. Promote sign-changing cases over large residual levels

Opposite-sign movements are immediately interpretable: the ledger selected changes whether the activity appears to expand or contract. These cases should open the empirical results. The heterogeneous US$40.07 billion FY2023 residual should remain secondary.

### 2. Build an explicit decision-consequence table

For every main result, report:

| Observed divergence | Possible mechanism | Decision affected | What the evidence establishes | What remains untested |
|---|---|---|---|---|

This prevents the manuscript from jumping directly from a measurement difference to a social harm.

### 3. Quantify policy-channel coverage carefully

Place the marketplace-share evidence beside the BPS all-channel decomposition, while preserving their different denominators. Ask what the marketplace reporting architecture covers legally and what commercial activity lies outside that particular route. Do not classify the remainder as unreported.

### 4. Complete revenue-component reconciliations

Extend the Tokopedia approach to every issuer with gross revenue, incentives, discounts, service categories, or issuer-provided comparable bases. The paper becomes stronger when it explains divergence rather than only detecting it.

### 5. Make institutional linkage falsifiable

Create a visibility matrix with separate fields for:

- record exists;
- actor holding the record;
- legal reporting obligation;
- operational implementation;
- identifier available for matching;
- verified use by the receiving institution; and
- observed outcome.

Absence of evidence in one field must remain `unknown`, not `no`.

### 6. Use the BPS evidence to test distribution, not only totals

If compatible published tables or authorized microdata permit, examine whether recordkeeping and channel choice differ by business size, sector, province, age, and owner characteristics. This would clarify where the information boundary is most economically important.

### 7. Tie each literature to one observed result

- financial comparability literature → issuer growth-signal divergence;
- platform economics and IFRS 15 → monetization and gross/net mechanisms;
- digital-measurement literature → BPS channels, participation, and vintage revision;
- third-party-information literature → seller-linked reporting architecture;
- informality and recordkeeping literature → participant-side capability differences.

The literature review should build the stakes rather than sit beside the empirics.

## Claims still outside the evidence

Even after adding stakes, the paper must not claim that it has estimated:

- missing GDP or national value added;
- undeclared merchant income;
- unpaid tax or tax evasion;
- causal investor losses;
- causal marketplace formalization;
- policy failure or success after PMK 37/2025;
- complete institutional non-linkage;
- welfare loss from fragmentation; or
- a representative ASEAN or global effect.

These are possible consequences or future tests, not current results.
