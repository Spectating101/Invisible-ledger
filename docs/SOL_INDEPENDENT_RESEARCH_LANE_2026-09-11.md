# Invisible Ledger — independent concept and empirical lane

**Status:** internal research memo. This file does not alter the advisor-approved state, select a final sample, or revise the authoritative proposal/manuscript. It is an independent synthesis and stress-test based on the current repository at commit `473a931`.

## 1. Core independent diagnosis

The project is strongest when framed neither as a tax-gap paper nor as a generic "different measures differ" paper. Its distinctive object is **distributed economic observability under digitalization**.

Digital commerce creates unusually rich traces of the same underlying commercial event, but those traces are generated and held by different actors and are transformed for different purposes. A transaction can simultaneously appear as:

- platform GMV/GTV/TPV;
- issuer revenue and incentives;
- merchant sales and financial records;
- payment-system records;
- BPS survey/statistical observations; and
- tax-administrative seller/turnover records.

The key research question is therefore not which ledger is "correct." It is:

> **When does changing the observed ledger change the economic conclusion about scale, growth, participation, or institutional visibility?**

This is a stronger and more falsifiable formulation than either the original residual story or a broad claim that the digital economy is "invisible."

## 2. Proposed conceptual object: the observability chain

The paper can treat visibility as a sequence rather than a binary state:

```text
commercial event
    -> digitally recorded
    -> structured by an intermediary
    -> conventionally documented by the participant
    -> publicly disclosed or statistically estimated
    -> legally reportable
    -> actually transmitted
    -> successfully linked
    -> usable for measurement / enforcement / finance
```

A record can clear one stage and fail the next. This explains why "digital traceability" and "economic visibility" are not synonyms.

The empirical contribution should be to identify which links are observed, which are only institutional possibilities, and where switching ledgers changes the substantive story.

## 3. Five candidate ideas worth preserving

### A. Ledger disagreement as the primary empirical phenomenon — CORE

The informative issuer result is not that transaction value exceeds revenue. It is that **transaction activity and recognized revenue can imply different directions or magnitudes of growth within the same issuer series**.

Tokopedia FY2022–FY2023 is the cleanest case because transaction value falls while third-party net segment revenue rises sharply. The component reconciliation then demonstrates that the divergence has interpretable mechanisms rather than being a purely rhetorical accounting gap.

This should remain the empirical entry point because it is easy to understand, directly testable, and finance-relevant.

### B. Visibility–growth mismatch — CORE / HIGH NOVELTY POTENTIAL

BPS creates a second, independent tension:

- marketplace activity is comparatively structured and marketplace users show stronger conventional recordkeeping/lower cash reliance;
- yet the 2023–2024 increase in BPS-measured e-commerce value is overwhelmingly concentrated in the non-marketplace component.

This supports a bounded but striking proposition:

> **The channel that appears more institutionally legible is not necessarily the channel where most recent measured growth occurs.**

This should be treated as a descriptive mismatch, not a causal trade-off. It is potentially the strongest "wow" result in the rebuilt paper because it connects growth, digital channel structure, and observability.

### C. Public disclosure topology / missingness — SECONDARY BUT DISTINCTIVE

Lazada and TikTok Shop are economically important in market-activity estimates but currently lack compatible public Indonesia revenue series. Their exclusion from the matched issuer panel is therefore not merely a nuisance.

A defensible secondary result is:

> **Market-activity visibility and matched public-accounting visibility are different coverage concepts.**

The study can report value coverage separately from paired-measure coverage without converting missing revenue into a constructed estimate.

This idea is useful because it turns source missingness into a documented property of the public information environment. It must not be generalized into a claim that private platforms intentionally obscure data or that non-disclosure causes bias in a known direction.

### D. Aggregation can change the apparent relationship — SECONDARY / METHODOLOGICAL

The province-level BPS evidence does not recover a stable marketplace–financial-record relationship, while BPS's published business-level analysis shows a clear association.

That contrast is not just a failed test. It illustrates that **the observational level itself affects what is empirically visible**.

This is especially coherent for a paper about measurement boundaries. It should be used as a methodological warning or robustness result, not promoted above the business-level evidence.

### E. Institutional linkage as a state transition, not a tax outcome — CORE INTERPRETIVE

PMK 37/2025 is valuable because it shows that seller identity and transaction-linked turnover are administratively distinct from marketplace corporate revenue.

The correct contribution is not "the government can now see everything" or "the old system missed tax." It is:

> **A legal mechanism can transform a privately structured platform record into a potentially administratively usable record, but legal reportability is not the same as operational transmission, matching, or measured compliance.**

This extends the observability chain and creates a clean future test once post-implementation data exist.

## 4. One underused case: Tokopedia/TikTok deconsolidation as a visibility discontinuity

The 2023–2024 Tokopedia/TikTok transaction is useful beyond ordinary perimeter control. Commercial activity can continue or expand while the listed parent's public accounting visibility changes because the entity leaves the consolidation perimeter and GoTo instead recognizes a contractual service fee.

This is a concrete illustration of a broader proposition:

> **Public observability can change because the reporting perimeter changes even when digital commercial activity does not disappear.**

This should be treated as a case illustration unless a clean quantitative continuity series is available. It is potentially more conceptually memorable than another generic limitation paragraph about acquisitions.

## 5. What should NOT become new headline constructs

Avoid inventing a new scalar "visibility index," "hidden economy ratio," or aggregate monetary residual from the current evidence. The data support a topology/architecture of observability better than a single universal score.

Avoid presenting the 42.94 percentage-point median divergence as the central economic magnitude. It is a useful sample diagnostic, but the small purposive set, early low revenue bases, and scope heterogeneity make it unsuitable as a population parameter.

Avoid making the BPS marketplace/non-marketplace growth difference into a causal substitution story. The current data establish composition and growth, not migration between channels.

Avoid treating PMK as proof of historical under-reporting. The legal mechanism is evidence about information architecture and administrative design.

## 6. Independent hierarchy of the final empirical story

If the advisor accepts a workable issuer boundary, the strongest sequence is:

1. **Issuer disagreement:** transaction activity and revenue can give materially different growth signals.
2. **Mechanism:** Tokopedia demonstrates how incentives/gross-versus-net structure can generate that disagreement; other issuer cases extend the mechanism set.
3. **Economy-wide widening:** BPS shows that broader Indonesian e-commerce growth has participation and channel dimensions not visible in issuer revenue.
4. **Visibility–growth mismatch:** marketplace users appear more conventionally documented, yet recent measured growth is concentrated outside the marketplace component.
5. **Institutional bridge:** PMK demonstrates how some digital records may become seller-linked administrative records, while operational linkage remains untested.
6. **External corroboration:** ASEAN and global issuer evidence demonstrate recurrence under heterogeneous definitions without being pooled into a common treatment effect.

The FY2023 Grab/Tokopedia/Shopee residual should remain lineage/sensitivity evidence rather than a central result.

## 7. Falsification and weakening conditions

The framework should state what would weaken the thesis:

- compatible transaction and revenue measures move closely together once perimeters are aligned;
- the apparent issuer divergences disappear under consistent revenue definitions;
- broader BPS channel differences disappear under corrected questionnaire/population concordance;
- matched participant-level evidence shows no meaningful recordkeeping/payment differences;
- official evidence establishes comprehensive cross-channel administrative linkage, reducing the fragmentation argument;
- disclosure missingness turns out to be trivial once compatible local-entity accounts are obtained.

A framework that can be weakened by better linkage evidence is stronger than one that defines every missing record as confirmation.

## 8. Proposal implication

The proposal does not need every idea above. For committee review, the minimum coherent story is:

> Digitalization creates multiple records of commerce. The study tests whether those records give the same account of Indonesian digital-commercial growth. Issuer evidence shows transaction activity and recognized revenue can diverge; BPS shows broader participation and channel growth that issuer accounts do not summarize; business-level BPS evidence shows marketplace participation is associated with stronger conventional records; and Indonesia's reporting architecture shows how seller-level transaction information can become administratively relevant. The modules remain separate because they observe different units and economic objects.

The sample-boundary decision remains prior to any claim that Blibli or Bukalapak are part of the final Indonesia core.

## 9. Suggested intellectual contribution language for later use

**Primary contribution:** a source-auditable multi-ledger reconciliation showing when conclusions about digital-commercial growth change across transaction, accounting, participant, statistical, and administrative records.

**Secondary contribution:** evidence that greater digital traceability does not imply a single unified public or administrative representation of the economy.

**Empirical tension:** the comparatively more structured marketplace channel is not where most recent BPS-measured national e-commerce growth occurred.

**Finance relevance:** issuer revenue and platform transaction metrics can convey different growth signals because monetization, incentives, principal/agent presentation, and perimeter determine how underlying commerce enters accounts.
