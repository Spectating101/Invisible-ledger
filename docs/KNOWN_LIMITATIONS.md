# Known limitations

- Grab Indonesia transaction value is unavailable as a direct country disclosure in the FY2023 construction; it is derived from Indonesia revenue using a group monetization rate.
- Shopee Indonesia transaction value relies on an external market estimate, while country revenue is derived using Sea's disclosed service monetization rate.
- Tokopedia is an Indonesia-aligned e-commerce segment, not an explicit geographic country line.
- Blibli third-party retail includes online travel through tiket.com and uses a published take-rate definition based on GPBD/TPV rather than net revenue/TPV.
- Bukalapak group disclosures include overseas operations; FY2024 transaction value and revenue have unequal reporting periods and must not be paired.
- Company-wide and segment histories do not validate Indonesia country allocations.
- Some quarterly disclosures are cumulative or appear in multiple publication vintages.
- Market-return analysis is exploratory; reviewed ledgers still contain unresolved contamination classifications.
- ASEAN countries have different platforms, tax systems, regulation and definitions. Regional context is not one pooled main sample.
- Public issuer documents and third-party market estimates vary in precision and definitions.
