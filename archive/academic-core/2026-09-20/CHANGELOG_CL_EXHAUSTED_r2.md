# The Constrained Ledger - Exhaustion pass r2 (2026-09-20)

This revision is a substantive research-development pass, not a formatting compression.

## Material changes
- Reframed the paper around **constraint closure**: evidence, issuance, risk adequacy, settlement, and governance are jointly necessary conditions, explicitly not claimed sufficient.
- Added **Proposition 1 - Passive-reference insufficiency**: an energy fact becomes constraining only when a declared rule maps it into a restriction on issuance, risk protection, or settlement.
- Retained and tightened the CEIR falsification: elapsed-days placebo, starting-stock sensitivity, changes-vs-levels failure, and inconclusive break evidence.
- Corrected the earlier overstatement that attribution and delivery never co-occur. Current EU guidance states that renewable PPAs are generally coupled with Guarantees of Origin.
- Replaced that claim with **Proposition 2 - Attribution-obligation coupling**: attribute evidence and delivery/payment obligations are distinct records that must be explicitly coupled across quantity, time, location, retirement, and shortfall.
- Added the **granularity-is-not-settlement** corollary and incorporated recent evidence on temporal matching of green-electricity claims.
- Recast the solar-pricing section as a model-adequacy test: internal agreement across pricing engines is not external validity; the ~12x-spot margin exposes process mismatch.
- Added a production-closure audit of the V2 workbench, distinguishing what the implementation demonstrates from operator-grade evidence still missing.
- Updated instrument classification for PPAs, EACs/GOs, granular certificates, power futures/future-to-spot mechanisms, and proof-of-work.
- Added explicit falsification conditions, research frontier, and archive/source-lineage statement.
- Retitled the subtitle to **When Does Energy Actually Bind a Digital Financial Claim?** to match the paper's actual evidentiary reach.
