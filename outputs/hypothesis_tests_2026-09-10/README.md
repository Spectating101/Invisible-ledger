# First hypothesis-test outputs

Generated by:

```bash
python3 scripts/analysis/build_hypothesis_tests.py
```

## Output files

| File | Grain and role |
|---|---|
| `indonesia_longitudinal_candidate_levels.csv` | 17 annual candidate levels across five platform series and three evidence tiers. |
| `indonesia_longitudinal_candidate_transitions.csv` | 12 consecutive within-series annual growth comparisons. |
| `indonesia_longitudinal_transition_summary.csv` | Growth-divergence diagnostics overall and by evidence tier. |
| `bps_national_levels.csv` | BPS national transaction, business-count, and derived component levels for 2022–2024. |
| `bps_national_growth_anatomy.csv` | Two national annual growth decompositions. |
| `bps_financial_report_timeline.csv` | Available national financial-report-ownership observations. |
| `bps_joint_status_bounds.csv` | Frechet bounds calculated from separate marketplace-use and recordkeeping marginals. |
| `bps_province_hypothesis_results.csv` | Two province cross-sections and one within-province-change association. |
| `bps_within_province_changes.csv` | 36 common-province changes from 2023–2024. |
| `data_quality_findings.csv` | Admission, scope, period, and ecological-inference risks. |
| `hypothesis_status.csv` | Claim-bounded status of the five proposed hypotheses. |

## Boundaries

- These outputs do not define an advisor-approved main sample.
- Candidate evidence tiers are never pooled as monetary totals.
- Tokopedia FY2021 and Bukalapak FY2024 are excluded for period mismatch.
- Blibli and Bukalapak remain scope-pending.
- Grab and Shopee remain conditional country reconstructions.
- BPS province results are ecological and do not identify business-level effects.
- No output measures missing GDP, unpaid tax, undeclared income, or tax evasion.
